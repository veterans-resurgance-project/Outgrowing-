I. Purpose & Scope of Operations

The purpose of this agreement is to link under-utilized rural acreage with trained, supervised next-generation agricultural growers. The Program Operator agrees to deploy disciplined field crews to establish, manage, and execute sustainable specialty crop or nursery production on the designated land parcel, removing all daily labor and management burdens from the Landowner.

II. Description of Land Asset & Boundaries

The Landowner grants permission to the Program Operator to utilize approximately acres of land located at the following address / description: . Operations will be strictly confined to these defined site boundaries to respect property privacy.

III. Crop Revenue & Yield Share Split

To ensure mutual economic revitalization, all harvests and wholesale specialty revenues generated from the designated acreage will be tracked and split according to the standard cooperative matrix:

  • 30% – 40% Landowner Allocation: Banked or delivered to the Landowner as a reliable secondary income stream for property maintenance.
  • 60% – 70% Trainee Capital Allocation: Retained directly by the program to build essential startup capital reserves and pay stipends for next-generation growers.
  • 5% – 10% Community Contribution: Allocated directly to local food banking boxes and senior nutrition networks to protect regional food security.

IV. Land Stewardship & Resource Protection

The Program Operator agrees to utilize extension-backed sustainable practices (partnering with regional Extension Research) to improve the property's long-term soil health. Crews will actively manage weeds, handle seasonal soil amendments, and install cover crops or overwintering procedures. This active agricultural use explicitly supports the land's ongoing farm-forest tax classification and compliance requirements.

V. Liability, Control, & Safety Parameters

The Program Operator maintains a strict professional project-management framework. All field trainees operate under direct command staff supervision at all times. The Program Operator assumes responsibility for safety compliance, equipment handling, and adheres to a strict zero-tolerance policy for hazardous or unauthorized activities on the property.
