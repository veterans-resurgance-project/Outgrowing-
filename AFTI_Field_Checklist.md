# AFTI Field Operations Field Guide: Mobile Biochar Production
## Print this checklist for your crew binders or clipboard during field deployments.

### 📋 Pre-Deployment & Site Setup Checklist
* [ ] **Trailer Inspection:** Verify tire pressure, wheel chocks, hitch lock, and stabilizing jacks.
* [ ] **Leveling & Stability:** Park on stable ground, drop jacks onto timber blocking, and ensure the trailer is level.
* [ ] **Fire Break & Perimeter:** Clear a 15-foot radius of dry duff, leaves, and surface debris. Establish wind direction.
* [ ] **Safety & Suppression Cache:**
  * [ ] Water IBC tote / pressure tanks filled & pressurized
  * [ ] Backpack water pumps & functional fire extinguishers staged
  * [ ] Hand tools (shovels, Pulaskis, metal rakes) ready
* [ ] **PPE Check:** P100 respirators, eye protection, leather gloves, and natural-fiber clothing verified for all crew members.
* [ ] **Feedstock Staging:** Sort and stack dry slash/wood within arm's reach of the kiln feed door (segregated by size).

### 🔥 Burn & Processing Workflow Checklist
* [ ] **Ignition Phase:** Establish kindling fire inside the kiln chamber to build an initial oxygen-starved draft.
* [ ] **Thermal Upscaling:** Feed larger split wood or forest slash progressively at a steady, measured pace.
* [ ] **Draft Regulation:** Monitor smoke output (transitioning from white/gray steam to translucent blue-gray pyrolysis gases).
* [ ] **Combustion Control:** Prevent complete ash conversion by tightly managing primary air intakes.

### 🌊 Quenching & Cooling Checklist
* [ ] **Oxygen Starvation:** Seal all primary air intakes completely once the chamber reaches capacity.
* [ ] **Water Quenching:** Apply metered water spray/mist directly into the chamber (wear respiratory PPE against steam).
* [ ] **Thermal Verification:** Confirm internal core temperatures are completely cold to the touch to eliminate any risk of spontaneous combustion.

### 📦 Bagging & Site Wrap-Up Checklist
* [ ] **Nutrient Charging (Optional):** Blend biochar with compost tea, mycorrhizae, or mineral dusts as needed.
* [ ] **Super-Sack Loading:** Secure the 1,000–1,500 lb agricultural super-sack beneath the discharge chute. Fill evenly.
* [ ] **Transport Staging:** Cinch/tie off the super-sack and transfer to the transport vehicle using tractor forks or tailgate lifter.
* [ ] **Final Site Police:** Sweep the footprint, secure trailer tie-downs, and verify zero active embers before leaving the landing.
