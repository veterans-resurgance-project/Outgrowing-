💧 Stage 5 Field Task: Low-Pressure Hillside Irrigation Assembly

📖 Field Operations Staging: Moving away from the potting bench, you carry a heavy coil of blank poly tubing out onto the slope. The upper springs provide water, but the old gravity-fed lines are cracked and filled with sediment. To keep the newly grafted trees alive without a motorized pump, the team must construct a low-pressure drip matrix. The elevation drop builds natural water pressure, but if you route the main line directly down the steep incline without a pressure regulator, the water pressure will blow the drip emitters right out of the plastic tubing. Your buddy stands ready with the line cutters and a crate of pressure-compensating fittings.

A mistake here will flood the hillside soil and wash away organic nutrients. How do you design and lay down the pipeline layout?
