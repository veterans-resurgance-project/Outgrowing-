Academic & Extension Partnerships

Bridging grassroots field operations with institutional science. We provide land-grant universities, extension services, and agricultural researchers with a scalable, real-world testbed for regional agronomy, soil restoration, and sustainable community infrastructure.

Mutual Value

What AFTI Delivers to Researchers

  • ▸
    Living Laboratories: Active Willamette Valley land-restoration sites available for soil biology, compost integration, and water management field trials.
  • ▸
    Empirical Field Data: Documented performance metrics derived from our workforce simulation models and active land-linking initiatives.
  • ▸
    Community Outreach Bridge: A direct execution pipeline translating academic research into practical, boots-on-the-ground community impact.
Collaboration Goals

What We Ask From Our Partners

  • ▸
    Technical & Scientific Advisory: Expert guidance on regional soil fertility, regenerative agronomy best practices, and water right protocols.
  • ▸
    Applied Research Pilots: Joint initiatives exploring municipal solid waste reuse, soil carbon sequestration, and post-wildfire watershed recovery.
  • ▸
    Educational Alignment: Support in anchoring our vocational training tracks to recognized regional agricultural standards.
