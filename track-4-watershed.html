Module Overview & Forestry Operations

Field training focused on post-wildfire hillside stabilization, contour wattling, check dam installation, hazardous ladder fuel thinning, and invasive species suppression across public timberlands in Linn County.

Unit 1: Post-Wildfire Erosion Control

Identifying hydrophobic soil crusts, constructing straw wattles along slope contours, and installing log check dams to trap sediment and prevent watershed mudslides.

Unit 2: Hazardous Fuels Reduction

Ladder fuel pruning, slash piling guidelines, and maintaining defensible space buffers around critical ecological and municipal infrastructure.
