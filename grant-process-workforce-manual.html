Staff Training Manual: Workforce Development

1. What This Grant Is & Why We Use It

State and Regional Workforce Development Grants fund career training, job creation, and apprenticeship pipelines that connect learners directly with local employers. For AFTI, this grant subsidizes our training-to-labor exchange, enabling us to provide trained, safety-certified recruits to participating host farms while building verified career portfolios for participants.

2. How the Operating Model Works in Practice

  • Earn-and-Learn Pipeline: Participants earn platform XP, verified credential badges, and shared harvest rights while working on host farms during their training phase.
  • Solving the Agricultural Labor Crunch: Host farms receive trained, motivated field crews without taking on unsustainable payroll risk during early plot development.
  • Verified Impact Portfolios: Every field hour, safety module completion, and labor contribution is recorded in a portable digital portfolio for future employment or funding reviews.

3. Step-by-Step Staff Operating Guide

  • Step 1 (Aggregate Signed Packages): Pull active, signed Cooperative Harvest & Maintenance Package agreements from the portal ledger showing participant commitment to host farm work plans.
  • Step 2 (Map Host Farm Capacity): Generate a roster mapping active recruits and stewards against participating partner farms by geographic location and required labor hours.
  • Step 3 (Quantify Economic Savings): Compile data highlighting labor cost reductions and productivity gains achieved across partner farms through our training cohorts.
  • Step 4 (Finalize Stipend & Safety Budget): Review budget allocations for student training stipends, field safety gear, insurance coverage, and coordinator oversight.
