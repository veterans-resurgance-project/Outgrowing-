🏡 Stage 4: Crossing the Threshold Into Housing

You have finally made it out of the cold and off the concrete. But having four walls also brings new temptations and tests. An old acquaintance from the streets knocks on your door, desperate for a warm place to sleep off the cold. Remembering your promise to help those left behind, you face a critical dilemma.

Your friend asks to crash on your floor for "just a couple of nights," but property lease rules strictly prohibit unauthorized guests, and their deep-seated struggles with substance volatility threaten your hard-won sanctuary. How do you respond?
