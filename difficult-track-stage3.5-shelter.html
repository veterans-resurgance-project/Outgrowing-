You feel the loss acutely, because when you have next to nothing, every single item is a lifeline. It becomes painfully clear: even when you make the best, most disciplined choices, the system can still deal you a brutal blow.

Shaken, hungry, and stripped of your emergency cash buffer, you stand outside the precinct into the cold night air. How do you recover your footing and press forward toward stage four?
