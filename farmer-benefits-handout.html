🚜 Cornerstone Anchor Host Benefits & Tax Overview

Partnering with the AFTI Initiative transforms idle agricultural space and distressed equipment into a thriving, community-supported vocational and agricultural hub. This overview details the direct financial, tax, and operational returns for property hosts.

📊 The 40 / 40 / 20 Shared Harvest Model

To ensure mutual prosperity, all yields from the anchor garden site are distributed transparently through a collaborative split:

40% Property Host
Guaranteed fresh, organic produce delivered directly to your door with zero labor required.
40% AFTI Project
Allocated to Core 5 trainers, trainee meals, and community educational workshops.
20% Food Bank
Donated to local food pantries/senior centers, eligible for host charitable tax tracking.

🛠️ Direct Operational & Financial Returns

Free Equipment Restoration

Our mechanical track diagnoses, repairs, and maintains your out-of-service tractors, tillers, and chainsaws at zero labor cost.

Zero-Labor Farm Yield

Enjoy a continuous, high-value harvest (valued up to $400+/month) managed entirely by our skilled Core 5 team and trainees.

Property Revitalization

Neglected barn space and acreage are cleaned, organized, and upgraded with modular raised beds and professional shop zones.

📋 Tax Advantages & Charitable Write-Offs

Participating landowners benefit from structured charitable frameworks designed to support agricultural producers and community contributors:

  • Charitable Contribution Deductions: The 20% portion of the harvest directed to qualified 501(c)(3) food banks can be documented as a charitable contribution originating from the anchor property's production.
  • Business Inventory & Produce Write-Offs: Tax provisions governing wholesome food donations and agricultural inventory allow producers to leverage deductible contributions. *(Consult your personal CPA or tax advisor for specific filing applications).*
  • Zero Out-of-Pocket Overhead: Because AFTI provides seeds, materials, and labor, you incur no deductible expenses while still receiving official documentation for community donations.
