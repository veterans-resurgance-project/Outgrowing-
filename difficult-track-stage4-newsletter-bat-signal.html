The Weekly Dispatch: Broadcasting the Bat-Signal

With the strategy mapped out on the greenhouse corkboard, the team formulates a two-fold mission: rescue the aging widow's historic wagon-train orchard, and lift up the most isolated waitlist grower who feels miles away from stability.

To pull this off, the team compiles their first official weekly dispatch. It reads like a modern agricultural bat-signal beamed out to the valley's network of hidden experts, shut-in dreamers, and quiet strategists. The bulletin outlines two burning problems of the week: correcting a severely compacted, acidic soil profile for the orchard, and designing a gravity-fed low-pressure irrigation rig that a single person with limited mobility can manage.

🛡️ The Administrative Screening Desk
As incoming solutions, micro-garden plans, and peer advice flood the ledger from anonymous handles across Linn and Benton counties, a designated station administrator steps in. They review every submission for clarity, safety, and editing requirements, ensuring that brilliant insights from quiet contributors are polished and ready to be woven directly into the team's operational playbook.

The broadcast is live, the screening desk is active, and the first wave of community solutions is arriving.
