🛠️ OSU Mitigation Playbook Directory

Select a category below to browse common agronomic challenges, environmental indicators, and standard OSU action steps.

Varroa Mite Surge / High Hive Mortality Risk

Indicator: Hive humidity > 70%, summer air temp > 85°F, mite count > 3% per 100 bees.

OSU Action Steps: Perform alcohol wash sampling. Apply formic acid or thymol treatments following Oregon State Beekeepers Association guidelines. Ensure bottom screen ventilation is fully open.

Nosema Spore Proliferation

Indicator: Damp spring conditions, substrate/hive internal humidity > 75%, fecal spotting on frames.

OSU Action Steps: Replace contaminated comb frames, feed colonies clean 2:1 sugar syrup with Fumagillin (if prescribed), and move hives to well-drained, sunlit southern slopes.

Pythium Root Rot / Damping Off

Indicator: Soil pH > 7.2, soil temp > 75°F, over-saturation in high-tunnel beds.

OSU Action Steps: Back off irrigation frequency, apply approved bio-fungicides (e.g., Trichoderma inoculants), and adjust water pH to 5.8–6.2 to optimize nutrient uptake and suppress pathogen spores.

Tipburn (Calcium Translocation Failure)

Indicator: Rapid growth under high humidity and inadequate airflow in leafy greens.

OSU Action Steps: Increase horizontal air circulation (HAF fans), maintain even soil moisture to prevent water stress, and supplement foliar calcium if tissue tests confirm deficiency.

Powdery Mildew on Specialty Blooms

Indicator: Night humidity spike (> 85%), stagnant canopy air, day/night temp swings > 25°F.

OSU Action Steps: Prune lower foliage for improved air penetration, apply preventative sulfur dust or potassium bicarbonate sprays, and space rows in accordance with OSU Extension field guides.

Fusarium Wilt in Cut Flower Beds

Indicator: Soil pH > 7.0 combined with high soil temperatures and yellowing lower leaves.

OSU Action Steps: Remove and destroy infected plant debris immediately. Implement a 4-year crop rotation schedule and solarize beds during peak July/August solar radiation windows.

Trichoderma Mold ("Green Mold") Contamination

Indicator: Substrate pH < 5.5 or > 7.0, excess moisture pooling on substrate blocks, white mycelium turning powdery green.

OSU Action Steps: Isolate contaminated blocks immediately to prevent spore drift. Sanitize growth chamber surfaces with a 10% bleach solution, verify pasteurization protocols, and maintain room humidity at 85-90%.

Stipe Elongation / Cap Abortions (CO2 Buildup)

Indicator: Air CO2 levels > 1,200 ppm, insufficient fresh air exchange (FAE) in fruiting rooms.

OSU Action Steps: Increase exhaust fan cycle frequency to achieve 4–6 complete room air exchanges per hour. Lower humidity slightly (2–3%) during flush maturation to prevent bacterial blotch.
