Question 1: Correct Answer is A

87 knots (100 mph)

Explanation: Part 107.51 sets the maximum speed limit for a small unmanned aircraft at 87 knots (100 mph).

Question 2: Correct Answer is B

8 hours

Explanation: Regulations strictly prohibit operating an sUAS within 8 hours of consuming any alcoholic beverage, or while under the influence.

Question 3: Correct Answer is C

Clear of clouds, 500 feet below and 2,000 feet horizontally away

Explanation: sUAS must stay clear of clouds, specifically maintaining 500 feet below and 2,000 feet horizontally clear.

Question 4: Correct Answer is B

10 days

Explanation: Any reportable incident resulting in serious injury, loss of consciousness, or property damage exceeding $500 must be reported to the FAA within 10 days.

Question 5: Correct Answer is B

The Remote Pilot in Command (PIC)

Explanation: The Remote PIC is directly responsible for and is the final authority on the operation of the sUAS.
