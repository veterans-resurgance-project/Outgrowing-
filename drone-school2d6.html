FAA Part 107 Cohort Training | Week 2, Day 6: Field Operations & Capstone

Target Audience: Veteran Cohort (Hillside Remediation & Agricultural Missions) Total Time: 4.5 Hours (1.5 hr mission briefing & safety audit, 3 hr live field capstone exercise)

I. Core Field Modules

Module 1: Pre-Deployment Mission Briefing & Risk Assessment (45 Mins)

  • Site Survey & Hazard Identification: Assessing local topography, power lines, wind patterns, and unauthorized ground personnel prior to launching heavy agricultural or mapping platforms.
  • Crew Briefing (Tailgate Meeting): Assigning explicit roles for the day—designating the Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC), Visual Observer (VO), and Payload Master.
  • Go/No-Go Decision Matrix: Reviewing weather forecasts, airspace notifications (TFRs/LAANC), and equipment logs to make a final operational sign-off.

Module 2: Live Field Execution & Multi-Payload Rotation (150 Mins)

  • Multispectral Mapping Flight: Executing an automated grid mission over a simulated burn scar to capture baseline NDVI index imagery.
  • Variable-Rate Seeding Run: Swapping payloads, verifying hopper calibration, and executing a targeted broadcast drop based on generated prescription shapefiles.
  • Contingency Drill Integration: Simulating an unexpected lost-link or low-battery emergency mid-mission to test crew response protocols.

Module 3: Post-Flight Debrief & Program Graduation (75 Mins)

  • Data Offloading & Telemetry Sync: Uploading flight logs, seed drop statistics, and processed maps to the central tracking database.
  • Cohort Debrief: Analyzing lessons learned, evaluating mission efficiency, and issuing internal cohort completion certificates.
