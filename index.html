Cultivating Agricultural Futures

The Agricultural Future & Training Initiative (AFTI) bridges the gap between under-utilized rural land and next-generation growers. We combine rigorous vocational pathways, custom advisory SOP alignment, and cooperative land-linking frameworks to deploy elite field teams that ensure mutual economic revitalization and regional food security across Linn County.

Have you ever dreamed of holding the keys to your own farm?

To feel the dirt in your hands, grow real food, and anchor your own independence? It feels completely impossible when the daily grind is a brutal battle just to survive—when missing documents, systemic loops, and a lack of capital block every door before you can even take a step.

Our simulation is built exactly for you. Start right where you are standing today. As you navigate choices, handle bureaucratic hurdles, and build team networks, the system registers your effort. Every milestone box you check accumulates points that build a real-world digital portfolio. Completion of these training steps creates verified metrics to help you qualify for land trust grants, farm placement matching programs, and institutional resources to turn your dream into reality.

We challenge you to select The Foster-Alum Route (The Difficult Path). See how your resilience locks in at the absolute absolute threshold of the journey. Outmaneuver systemic cracks, coordinate field dispatches, and protect your team to beat the hard winter weather and the bank boss in the final high-stakes battle!
