Cultivating Agricultural Futures

The Agricultural Future & Training Initiative (AFTI) bridges the gap between under-utilized rural land and next-generation growers. We combine rigorous vocational pathways, custom advisory SOP alignment, and cooperative land-linking frameworks to deploy elite field teams that ensure mutual economic revitalization and regional food security across Linn County.
