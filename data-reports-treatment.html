Mushroom Cultivation Task List

Focus: Humidity control, misting cycles, CO2 exhaust, and contamination checks.

Daily Routines

Monitor and log fruiting chamber humidity (target 85–92%) and temperature.
Inspect automated misting cycles; manually mist block surfaces if dry.
Run exhaust fans / check passive air exchange to purge CO2 build-up.
Morning & evening harvest inspection (pick before cap edges curl upward).

Weekly Routines

Inspect incubation room grain spawn / sawdust blocks for trichoderma mold.
Clear out exhausted blocks, compost spent substrate, and sanitize shelving.
Package harvested mushrooms into vented containers and stage in cold storage (34–38°F).

Apiary & Pollination Task List

Focus: Colony health, water access, swarm prevention, and varroa monitoring.

Daily Routines

Observe morning flight activity at hive entrances (pollen loads & orientation).
Check and refill apiary water stations (ensure clean floats).
Quick visual scan for ant trails, yellowjacket pressure, or hive beetle entry.

Weekly Routines

Full 7–10 day spring/summer hive inspection (check for queen cells & brood pattern).
Conduct Varroa mite monitoring (sticky board count or alcohol wash).
Build frames, wire foundations, or harvest capped honey supers.
