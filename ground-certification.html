What is Ground Person Certification?

The Ground Person Certification validates your foundational training in forestry safety, chipper operations, rigging support, hazardous fuels management, and site communication. It is an essential credential for working safely alongside fallers and heavy equipment operators on watershed restoration and wildfire recovery projects.

Governing Standards: Complies with Oregon OSHA (Division 7 Forest Activities) safety mandates and regional professional logger training frameworks.
