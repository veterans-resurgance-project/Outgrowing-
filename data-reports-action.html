Monitor substrate moisture and block humidity. Ensure air exchange cycles run every 4 hours. Log relative humidity percentage and any green mold indicators immediately.

🛠️ Mitigation Playbook & Guides

Trichoderma (Green Mold) Spore Outbreak

Contamination on substrate blocks during incubation phase.

Protocol: Isolate block immediately, spray 3% hydrogen peroxide solution on localized spot, increase fresh air exchange.

Substrate Drying / Stipe Cracking

Inadequate ambient tent humidity causing premature cap drying.

Protocol: Check ultrasonic fogger nozzles, adjust misting timer frequency from 15 min to 25 min intervals.

Varroa Mite Threshold Exceeded

Alcohol wash screening shows >3 mites per 100 bees.

Protocol: Apply organic oxalic acid vaporization or thymol pads according to seasonal temperature guidelines.

Low Feed Reserves in Super

Unseasonable cold snap halting forage nectar flow.

Protocol: Install internal 2:1 sugar syrup feeder frame immediately to prevent starvation risk.

Public Land Trail / Brush Restoration

Clearing invasive species and wildfire fuel reduction lanes.

Protocol: Wear required PPE (gloves, eye protection), stack slash piles according to ODF forestry clearance guidelines, and log exact participant hours.

Site Clean-up & Waste Sorting

Municipal solid waste infrastructure support and sorting.

Protocol: Separate recyclables, organic compostables, and landfill refuse into designated bins. Record total weight or bag count.
