🔍 Step-by-Step Diagnostic Guides

Click any issue below to review field containment and remediation procedures:

🍄 Trichoderma (Green Mold)

Symptom: White mycelium turns aggressive dark green powdery patches on substrate blocks.

Remediation Step: 1. Isolate infected block immediately to prevent spore spread. 2. Mist spot with 3% hydrogen peroxide solution. 3. Increase fresh air exchange (FAE) and verify room humidity does not stagnate.

🐝 Varroa Mite Threshold Exceeded

Symptom: >3 mites per 100 bees on sticky board or visible deformed wing virus.

Remediation Step: 1. Apply organic formic acid or oxalic acid vapor treatment per seasonal protocol. 2. Re-test hive mite load after 14 days. 3. Consider drone brood interruption if population persists.

🌿 Powdery Mildew / Aphids

Symptom: White powdery spots on leaf surfaces or clustered sticky sap-suckers on stem tips.

Remediation Step: 1. Spray affected foliage with potassium bicarbonate or diluted neem oil solution at dusk. 2. Improve canopy spacing to lower humidity microclimates. 3. Release beneficial ladybugs if in greenhouse zone.
