🌰 Orchard Hazelnuts: Ergonomics & Harvest Techniques

Safe operating procedures for orchard gathering, ground recovery, tool handling, and back strain mitigation.

1. Primary Physical Risk Factors

  • Repetitive Stooping & Ground Gathering: Manual sweep recovery requires rapid, repeated waist bending.
  • Vibration Stress: Handheld mechanical limb-shakers or backpack sweepers cause Hand-Arm Vibration Syndrome (HAVS).
  • Neck Hyperextension: Looking up into tree canopies during mechanical shaking or manual pruning.

2. Biomechanical & Ergonomic Standards

Always utilize long-handled orchard rakes and rolling nut gatherers to keep the spine neutral. Mandate ISO-certified anti-vibration gloves when operating backpack sweepers or motorized equipment.

3. Step-by-Step Harvest Protocol

  1. Canopy Sweep & Clear: Clear drop zones around trunk bases using long rakes while maintaining an upright spine.
  2. Center-Out Gathering: Rake nuts into central windrows along tree rows rather than scattered piles.
  3. Two-Person Bin Lift: Bend knees to 90 degrees, keep totes flush against the body, and lift on joint count.
