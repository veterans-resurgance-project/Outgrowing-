FAA Part 107 Cohort Training | Day 3: Broadcast & Seeding Payloads

Target Audience: Veteran Cohort (Hillside Remediation & Agricultural Missions) Total Time: 4.5 Hours (2.0 hr morning mechanics, 2.5 hr field calibration & live drop simulation)

I. Core Lecture Modules

Module 1: Agricultural & Restoration Spreading Mechanics (75 Mins)

  • Granular Spreader Systems: Operating high-capacity agricultural spreading payloads (e.g., DJI Agras T-series spreading tanks) for cover crop seeding and fertilizer broadcast.
  • Particle Ballistics & Wind Drift: Factoring in seed weight, shape, density, and local crosswinds to calculate dispersion drift across steep slopes.
  • Swath Width Calibration: Determining effective spread width based on flight height (typically 3 to 5 meters AGL for agricultural broadcast) to prevent striping or gaps.
  • Material Flow Control: Calibrating gate opening sizes and disc RPM to match target application rates (e.g., 30–100 lbs/acre depending on prescription maps).

Module 2: Pre-Flight Payload Inspection & Weight Limits (75 Mins)

  • Maximum Takeoff Weight (MTOW): Strict adherence to aircraft structural limits and Part 107 weight category thresholds when handling heavy payloads.
  • Center of Gravity (CG) Verification: Ensuring payload hopper weight is evenly distributed to prevent motor strain, unstable flight dynamics, or attitude control failure.
  • Emergency Hopper Jettison: Knowing manual and automated emergency release protocols if an engine failure or power loss occurs mid-flight with a heavy load.
  • Pre-Flight Mechanical Check: Inspecting augers, spinner disks, gate actuators, and quick-release pins for jamming or debris buildup.

Module 3: Live Field Execution & Post-Mission Logging (45 Mins)

  • Crew Resource Management (CRM): Coordinating roles between the Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC), Visual Observer (VO), and Payload Ground Crew during active spreading operations.
  • Telemetry & Task Logging: Recording total seed weight dispensed, area covered (acres), and flight logs to sync with watershed tracking databases.
