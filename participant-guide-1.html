# OUTGROWING-AG: PARTICIPANT VERIFICATION POLICY & SUBMISSION GUIDE ## 1. Purpose & Compliance Overview To ensure compliance with institutional grant requirements, state reporting standards, and funding agreements, Outgrowing-Ag utilizes a structured verification process for specific demographic tracks (including veterans, former foster alumni, individuals with disabilities, and underemployed participants). This process ensures program integrity, prevents system abuse, and guarantees that grant-funded resources reach the communities they are intended to support, while strictly protecting participant privacy. --- ## 2. Status Categories & Acceptable Documentation When completing your digital onboarding intake, selecting a specific qualifying status requires providing corresponding documentation into your secure participant folder. * **Veterans / Military Service Members** * *Accepted Verification:* Copy of DD-214 (Member-4 copy preferred), VA Certificate of Eligibility, or official military ID card. * *Privacy Note:* Please redact or black out Social Security Numbers before uploading. * **Former Foster Youth / Independence Track** * *Accepted Verification:* State or county Department of Human Services (DHS) verification letter, independent living program documentation, or tribal ward verification. * **Individuals with Disabilities / Vocational Access** * *Accepted Verification:* Documentation of active service connection or support through State Vocational Rehabilitation agencies (e.g., Oregon Vocational Rehabilitation), SSI/SSDI benefit verification letter, or standard ADA accommodation documentation. * **Underemployed / Low-Income Status** * *Accepted Verification:* Recent pay stubs, W-2 forms, unemployment documentation, or a signed self-certification of household income relative to Area Median Income (AMI) guidelines. --- ## 3. Secure Submission & Redaction Protocol To maintain strict data security and prevent unnecessary data storage: 1. **Redact Sensitive Information:** Prior to uploading any document to your folder, black out or remove sensitive personal identifiers (such as Social Security Numbers, exact medical diagnostic details, or banking data). We only require verification of your *status*, not your private personal history. 2. **Secure Folder Drop:** Upload your redacted document directly into your designated secure onboarding sub-folder within the platform. 3. **The "Verified" Status Stamp:** Once reviewed by compliance personnel, your profile will be updated with a secure **"Verified"** system flag, unlocking your specialized milestone tracks. Raw documentation is handled according to strict organizational data privacy protocols. --- ## 4. Questions & Support If you experience difficulties securing documentation or have questions about which category applies to your situation, submit a routing note through your platform folder system to connect with our administrative support team for guidance.
