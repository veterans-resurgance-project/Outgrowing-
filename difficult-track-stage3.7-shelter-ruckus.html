⚡ Stage 3.7: The Midnight Ruckus & Shifting Ground

💥 Narrative Event: The Limits of the Net You did everything right. For twelve straight days, you hit curfew, kept your area immaculate, and coordinated flawlessly with your housing navigator. But at 2:00 AM on your final night, the fragile peace of the communal room shatters. A violent fight erupts between two residents near the lockers. Shouted threats echo off the cinderblock walls, lockers are slammed, and the overhead fluorescent lights are thrown on as staff frantically call for emergency backup.

In the terrifying rush and chaos of everyone scrambling for safety, your small living perimeter is trampled. By the time the police arrive to clear the room and calm the tension, you look down in disbelief—someone used the distraction to steal a spare jacket and your neatly organized folder of printed library training confirmations.

Heart pounding, your voice shakes as you try to explain the theft to an exhausted officer filling out a report. He doesn't even look up from his clipboard. He just waves you off with a cold, dismissive sigh: "Look, kid, it's a crowded room. You should take better care of your things. We're not tracking down a missing folder tonight."

The bitter sting of injustice burns in your chest. You followed every rule perfectly, yet you are still the one left holding the loss, ignored by the systems meant to protect you. How do you anchor your resolve in the face of this setback?
