Module Overview & Field Protocols

This track establishes foundational competency in sustainable high-tunnel management, soil texture analysis, organic amendment dosing, and passive moisture balancing. Recruits will master testing soil composition to match optimal crop yield parameters across Willamette Valley microclimates.

Unit 1: Soil Texture & Composition

Understanding the ratio of sand, silt, and clay. Field testing via ribbon tests and jar settling methods to evaluate drainage rates, cation exchange capacity (CEC), and organic matter content.

Unit 2: Micro-Irrigation & Fertigation

Configuring pressure-compensating drip tape, liquid organic fertilizer injection rates, and moisture sensor threshold triggers to prevent nutrient leaching and root saturation.
