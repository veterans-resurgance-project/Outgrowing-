SOP #03 • Volunteer Training Module

Native Seed Collection, Cleaning & Storage

Target Window: August 15 – October 31 (Seed Maturity Window)

Volunteer XP +100 XP

Event Overview & Objective

Gathering locally adapted native grass, forb, and flower seeds preserves genetic diversity ideal for regional burn scar recovery. Proper harvesting techniques ensure seeds are mature and protected from mold and pest damage.

Harvest Rules

  • NEVER use plastic bags (traps humidity and causes mold)
  • Collect max 5-10% of seeds from any single plant stand
  • Verify species ID to avoid propagating invasive weeds

Required Tools & PPE

  • Paper collection bags & burlap sacks
  • Hand snips / pruners & collection buckets
  • Wiremesh cleaning screens & sieves
  • Waterproof markers & weather-proof tags

Step-by-Step Collection & Cleaning Protocol

1. Test Seed Readiness Gather on dry afternoons. Seed heads should be brown, dry, and papery. Seeds should separate easily from the stalk when rubbed gently between gloved fingers.
2. Collection Motion & Tagging Gently strip seed heads into paper bags or clip seed pods using hand pruners. Label every bag immediately with: Species, Harvest Location, Elevation, Date, and Collector Name.
3. Air Drying (24-48 Hours) Spread harvested seed heads onto screens or kraft paper in a warm, dry, well-ventilated room. Stir daily to ensure uniform drying.
4. Winnowing & Cleaning Rub dried seed heads over wire mesh screens to separate pure seeds from chaff and leaves. Store clean seed in airtight containers with desiccant packs in a cool, dark room.
