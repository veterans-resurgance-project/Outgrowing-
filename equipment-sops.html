Equipment, Machinery & Safety SOPs

Standard operating procedures for pre-trip inspections, power take-off (PTO) safety, and lockout/tagout protocols.

1. Pre-Trip Inspection Protocols

Before any tractor, implement, or heavy transport vehicle is operated on site or public right-of-ways, the operator must complete a physical walk-around check:

  • Fluid Levels: Check engine oil, coolant, hydraulic fluid reservoirs, and transmission levels on flat ground.
  • Tires & Wheels: Inspect for sidewall cracking, proper inflation pressure, and secure lug nut torque.
  • Safety Shields & Guards: Verify that all master shields, belt guards, and rotating component covers are securely bolted in place.
  • Lighting & SMV Emblem: Ensure headlights, flashers, turn signals, and the Slow Moving Vehicle (SMV) triangle are clean and fully functional.

2. Core Safety Infrastructure

PTO Safety Mandates

Power Take-Off shafts rotate at high speeds with lethal torque potential. Never step across a rotating PTO shaft. Always disengage the PTO, shut down the engine, and wait for all motion to cease before clearing clogs or servicing attached implements.

Lockout / Tagout (LOTO)

When performing maintenance, blade sharpening, or gear servicing, isolate energy sources. Turn off the master ignition, remove the key, engage parking brakes, and apply physical lockout tags to prevent accidental startup.

Critical Operator Warning

Never bypass safety interlocks, seat safety switches, or shielding guards to save time. Equipment accidents involving unshielded rotating shafts or hydraulic pinhole leaks carry catastrophic injury risks.
