# FAA Part 107 Cohort Training | Day 1: Regulations & Operations * **Target Audience:** Veteran Cohort (Hillside Remediation & Agricultural Missions) * **Total Time:** 4.5 Hours (2.5 hr morning block, 2 hr afternoon practical/scenario block) --- ## I. Core Lecture Modules ### Module 1: The Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC) & Certification (75 Mins) * **Legal Accountability:** * The Remote Pilot in Command (RPIC) holds ultimate responsibility and final authority for the operation of the sUAS (14 CFR Section 107.19). * **Certification Requirements:** * Must be at least 16 years old. * Must pass the initial aeronautical knowledge test (Unmanned Aircraft General - Small, or UAG). * Must successfully pass a TSA background security vetting check. * Must hold a physical or digital Remote Pilot Certificate in their possession during flight operations. * **Registration & Remote ID:** * All aircraft over 250 grams must be registered via **FAA DroneZone**. * Standard **Remote ID** compliance is mandatory, broadcasting drone identity, GPS location, and velocity in real-time. * **Fitness for Flight (IMSAFE Checklist):** * **I**llness: Do not fly if sick. * **M**edication: Check side effects (no flying under sedating medications). * **S**tress: Psychological pressures affecting cognitive focus. * **A**lcohol: Zero tolerance; must be 8 hours "bottle to throttle" and blood alcohol concentration below 0.04%. * **F**atigue: Mental or physical exhaustion. * **E**motion: High emotional distress or distraction. ### Module 2: Operating Rules & Airspace Boundaries (75 Mins) * **Visual Line of Sight (VLOS) Mandate:** * The RPIC or Visual Observer (VO) must be able to see the aircraft throughout the entire flight unaided by any device except corrective lenses (glasses/contacts). * *Note:* First-Person View (FPV) goggles alone do not satisfy the legal VLOS requirement. * **Flight Envelopes & Limits (Part 107.51):** * **Maximum Altitude:** 400 feet AGL (Above Ground Level), *except* when flying closer than 400 feet to a structure, where you may fly up to 400 feet above the structure’s topmost boundary. * **Maximum Speed:** 87 knots (100 mph ground speed). * **Minimum Visibility:** 3 statute miles from the control station. * **Cloud Clearances:** Must remain 500 feet below clouds and 2,000 feet horizontally from clouds. * **Right-of-Way Rules:** * Small unmanned aircraft **must yield the right-of-way to all manned aircraft** at all times (airplanes, helicopters, crop dusters). Yielding means giving way and altering course immediately. * **Operations Over People and Moving Vehicles:** * Categorized under Categories 1–4 depending on weight, kinetic energy impact thresholds, and FAA declarations. Sustained flight directly over open-air assemblies of people is generally prohibited without specific category compliance or waivers. ### Module 3: Mishap Reporting & Mission Scenarios (45 Mins) * **Mandatory FAA Reporting (Section 107.9):** * Any sUAS accident must be reported to the FAA (via online portal or local FSDO) **within 10 days** if it results in: 1. **Serious injury** to any person or any loss of consciousness. 2. **Property damage** exceeding **$500** to repair or replace to its original state (calculated excluding the value of the drone itself). * **Contextual Application to Cohort:** * *Hillside Burn Scars:* Steep terrain gradients cause ground elevation to drop rapidly away from the pilot, making it easy to bust the 400-foot AGL ceiling or lose VLOS behind ridges. * *Agricultural Sectors:* Low-altitude valley operations require active scanning for low-flying agricultural sprayers and crop dusters. --- ## II. End-of-Day Diagnostic Pretest ### Question 1: Visual Line of Sight You are conducting an environmental scan of a steep hillside burn scar. Your drone dips down into a ravine, completely disappearing behind a rocky ridge from your physical vantage point, though you maintain connection via your FPV screen. Is this flight legal under Part 107? * A) Yes, as long as the telemetry signal and video feed remain active. * B) No, because visual line of sight (VLOS) must be maintained by the remote pilot or visual observer unaided by any device other than corrective lenses. * C) Yes, provided you notify local air traffic control before breaking line of sight. > **Correct Answer: B** ### Question 2: Accident Reporting While operating a heavy multispectral mapping drone over an agricultural research plot, you lose control momentarily, causing the aircraft to collide with a farmer's specialized irrigation pump house. The structural damage totals roughly $750. What is your obligation under FAA regulations? * A) No report is required unless someone was hurt. * B) Report the incident to the local sheriff within 48 hours. * C) Report the accident to the FAA portal or local FSDO within 10 days. > **Correct Answer: C** ### Question 3: Night Operations You plan to launch a pre-dawn thermal mission to assess hillside moisture and animal movement before sunrise. Under Part 107, what specific equipment must your drone and control station feature for night operations? * A) High-intensity landing floodlights pointing straight down. * B) Anti-collision lighting visible for at least 3 statute miles, with a flash rate sufficient to prevent spatial disorientation. * C) A certified aviation radio tuned to local tower frequencies. > **Correct Answer: B**
