Welcome to the AFTI Trade Training Program

The AFTI Portal provides rigorous, hands-on preparation for commercial drone operations, regulatory compliance, and post-wildfire watershed restoration initiatives in the Pacific Northwest.

Prospective operators and visitors are welcome to explore our public curriculum outline and official educational resources below. To track progress, unlock interactive pretests, and use the field telemetry tools for free, simply click any button to sign in.
