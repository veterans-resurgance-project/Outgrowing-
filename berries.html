🍓 Diversified Berries: Ergonomics & Harvest Techniques

Standard operating procedures for efficient caneberry and strawberry harvesting, injury prevention, and quality control.

1. Primary Physical Risk Factors

  • Repetitive Pinch-Grip Fatigue: Sustained two-finger pincer posture causes wrist and flexor tendon strain.
  • Lumbar Flexion (Lower Back Strain): Continuous forward stooping when harvesting low-canopy fruit.
  • Sun & Heat Exposure: Prolonged overhead exposure leading to early fatigue and reduced hand-eye coordination.

2. Biomechanical & Ergonomic Standards

Pinch fruit stems with the pad of the thumb and side of the index finger rather than fingertips. Always maintain a wide base of support, hinge at the hips, or drop to one knee using a knee pad. Keep flats elevated on field stands between waist and hip height.

3. Step-by-Step Harvest Protocol

  1. Scan & Select: Scan the cane from top to bottom before reaching into the canopy.
  2. Roll-and-Snap: Place thumb behind the berry calyx and gently roll upward; do not yank straight off the stem.
  3. Two-Handed Rhythmic Harvesting: One hand gently holds/clears the leaf canopy while the primary hand plucks.
