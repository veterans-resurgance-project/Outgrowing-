Module 1 Training Syllabus

High-Tunnel & Controlled Environment Basics

+250 XP Reward

This foundational module covers the structural mechanics, microclimate regulation, and passive solar management required for high-tunnel agricultural production. Operators will learn how to monitor internal temperature swings, manage roll-up side ventilation, and configure baseline drip irrigation zones.

Core Learning Objectives

  • Understanding structural anchoring and wind-load tolerances for hoop houses.
  • Managing thermal mass and passive heat retention cycles.
  • Preventing humidity spikes and fungal propagation through proper cross-ventilation.
