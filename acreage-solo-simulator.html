⚖️ The Side-by-Side Reality: Teamwork vs. Winging It

🤝 The Shared Harvest Advantage

Farmers who leaned into cooperative partnerships benefited from shared bulk equipment, vetted seasonal labor pools, and pre-negotiated B2B distribution. They experienced lower stress, steady compounding growth, and built-in climate risk mitigation.

⚡ The Independent Solo Lessons

Farmers who chose the solo grind discovered immense autonomy and high initial capital ($25k), but battled higher turnover overhead, equipment maintenance fatigue, and unpredictable market swings without co-op backing.
