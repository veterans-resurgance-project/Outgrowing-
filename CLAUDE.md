# OUTGROWING-AG SYSTEM CONSTITUTION (`CLAUDE.md`)
*Project Scope: The Agricultural Future & Training Initiative (AFTI)*
*Core Principle: Balance empathy with raw, direct operational truth.*

---

## 📊 1. Core Financial & Fiduciary Parameters
To ensure project expansion and long-term grant funding eligibility, the fiscal architecture must align strictly with these numbers:

*   **Pilot Program Trainee Allocation Split:**
    *   **$1,100 Active Draw:** Immediate monthly stipend distribution to cover essential housing stability, transportation credits, and food lines.
    *   **$1,900 Deferred Compensation:** Delayed disbursement banked securely in local credit union reserves. This fund accumulates to provide foundational startup capital for independent land placements at graduation.
*   **Operational Funding Stance:**
    *   **100% Contracted Heavy Equipment:** Zero initial capital equipment leasing. All heavy machinery needs are contracted out at flat local rates to support local operators and eliminate massive debt traps.
    *   **Symbiotic Program Model:** Trainees provide high-value specialty crop labor and post-wildfire watershed remediation for aging landowners in exchange for an equitable share-crop percentage (30%–40%).

---

## 🏛️ 2. Governance Matrix & Board Structure
To protect program integrity and maintain rapid, lean decision-making:

*   **The Lean Board Model:** Controlled by exactly three active advisory board members and a dedicated corps of volunteers specializing in IT, website management, and outreach.
*   **Independent Oversight:** Utilizes an independent governance board to ensure transparent fiscal reporting and absolute audit readiness for federal grant narrations.
*   **Background Management Style:** Leadership shares credits with volunteers and operates from a background position, prioritizing team resilience over top-heavy corporate hierarchy.

---

## 🎨 3. UI Framework & Technical Constraints
All software branches must adhere to this vanilla web design playbook:

*   **The Stack:** 100% framework-free HTML5, modern CSS (using native variables and dark mode defaults), and clean vanilla JavaScript.
*   **Data Portability:** Uses `localStorage` parsing arrays rather than complex server databases, keeping the platform fully functional on local devices or offline memory sticks.
*   **Design Theme Elements:** Public front-facing pages leverage a visually rich, appealing format. Title and cover pages features a stylized swan graphic sitting on top of clean layout borders.
*   **Non-Punitive Design:** Incorrect or poor choices inside the simulation must award points (e.g., Middle Choice = 25 points, Bad Choice = 0 points) along with a clear, direct explanation of the real-world consequences, guiding them back onto an active recovery path.
