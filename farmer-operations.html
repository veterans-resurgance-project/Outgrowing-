🚜 Farmer & Grower Operations & Support Wish List

Designed directly from regional grower feedback in the Willamette Valley. This track bridges practical farm execution, labor deployment, soil health chemistry, and water infrastructure management.

Core Curriculum & Grower Support Modules

Module 301: Seasonal Crop Timing & Field Workflow

Master OSU-aligned planting calendars, succession bed prep schedules, and efficient day-to-day farm labor management.

Module 302: Cooperative Equipment Handling & Maintenance

Safe operation, routine multi-point pre-checks, and shared upkeep protocols for tractors, tillers, and implements.

Module 303: Harvest Logistics & Cold-Chain Staging

Quality control, hydro-cooling, washing, sorting, and cold-chain staging standards for local food hubs.

Grower Wish List Priority

Module 304: Soil Fertility, Testing & Organic Amendment SOPs

Standardized soil sampling protocols, C:N ratio balancing, cover cropping strategies, and organic amendment calculations.

Grower Wish List Priority

Module 305: Irrigation Infrastructure & Water Management

Drip line pressure regulation, main-line winterization, moisture sensor placement, and water conservation efficiency.

Grower Wish List Priority

Module 306: Integrated Pest & Weed Management (IPM)

Mechanical weed suppression, beneficial insect habitat corridors, scouting thresholds, and organic pest barriers.
