Veteran Crew Task & Remediation Checklists

Complete daily and weekly safety musters, erosion control routines, and habitat targets on clipboards or digitally before logging your shift hours below.

Crew Safety & Pre-Deployment Muster

Focus: Personal protective equipment (PPE), hazard checks, communications, and daily briefings.

Daily Routines

Conduct 0800 crew roll call, fitness-for-duty check, and hydration status verification.
Inspect PPE (boots, hard hats, eye protection, high-vis gear, and chainsaw chaps).
Test field radios, satellite communicators, and emergency GPS beacon check-ins.
Review daily weather forecast, wind speeds, and local fire/slope hazard alerts.

Weekly Routines

Inspect first-aid trauma kits, eye-wash stations, and vehicle emergency response packs.
Perform vehicle and tool safety audits (chainsaws, hand tools, rigging straps, and fluids).

Burn Scar & Erosion Control Task List

Focus: Hydrophobic soil mitigation, wattles, contour felling, and runoff barriers.

Daily Routines

Assess burn scar sections for active rockfall risks and unstable ash pits.
Install straw wattles and coir logs securely anchored along slope contour lines.
Construct hand-built check dams in minor gullies using downed woody debris.

Weekly Routines

Inspect previously installed sediment barriers following weather events; repair breaches.
Apply certified weed-free mulch and native seed mixes to vulnerable high-slope scars.

Riparian Buffer & Habitat Restoration

Focus: Stream bank stabilization, invasive weed removal, and native planting.

Daily Routines

Monitor stream crossing points for bank slumping or accelerated siltation.
Targeted removal of aggressive invasive weeds (blackberry, scotch broom, knotweed).
Water and inspect newly established riparian saplings and live stakes.

Weekly Routines

Install protective browse cages or trunk guards around vulnerable young hardwoods.
Clear debris jams threatening culverts while preserving beneficial instream large wood.

Grant Compliance & Field Data Logging

Focus: Labor hours tracking, in-kind match records, and GIS sample logging.

Daily Routines

Log crew man-hours, volunteer participation counts, and equipment usage times.
Record GPS coordinates and photo points for completed remediation sectors.
Compile daily field notes into the centralized digital ledger.

Weekly Routines

Reconcile in-kind matching hour logs against grant milestone deliverables.
Export weekly telemetry report for administrative review and partner reporting.
