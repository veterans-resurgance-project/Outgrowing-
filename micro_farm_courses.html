Square-Foot Gardening & Companion Architecture

Rooted in high-density urban and small-scale growing principles, this curriculum teaches participants how to maximize yields in minimal space using precise companion planting, succession timing, and multi-tier vertical staging.

📐 1. The Grid Layout

Dividing beds into strict 1-foot squares. Assigning exact plant counts per square (1 large, 4 medium, 9 compact, or 16 micro) to eliminate wasted soil space.

🌿 2. Companion Synergy

Pairing deep-root and shallow-root varieties alongside natural pest deterrents (e.g., marigolds, herbs, alliums) to create self-defending micro-ecosystems.

🔄 3. Continuous Rotation

Immediate succession sowing the moment a crop is harvested, paired with soil amendment additions to prevent nutrient depletion without heavy tractor tilling.

Community Garden "Rescue" & Challenge Submission

Local gardeners submit their micro-farm layout attempts and roadblocks. Under our Pay-It-Forward Reciprocity Clause, selected rescue sites receive an on-site visit from a 5-person cell in exchange for 1 day of volunteer service back to the program.

Pay-It-Forward Reciprocity Agreement "I agree that if my garden is selected for a 5-person cell rescue visit and consultation, I will commit 1 day of volunteer service back to the Outgrowing-Ag program (such as biochar production support or public land restoration assistance) in exchange for free expert training and remediation."
