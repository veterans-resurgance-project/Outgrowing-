Research, Engineering & Academic Synergy

Pairing engineering, environmental science, and agricultural disciplines (leveraging local networks like LBCC and OSU Extension) with hands-on field trials to bridge lab-grade science with boots-on-the-ground execution.

⚡ The Institutional Symbiosis Model

Academic programs often seek real-world field sites for student capstones and telemetry testing, while operational hubs need rigorous data hygiene and scientific validation. By embedding student researchers directly into our volunteer and watershed training sessions, we create a mutual multiplier: students gain authentic lab and field access while substantially boosting program engagement metrics and grant-readiness documentation.

🌡️

1. Soil Telemetry & Tracking

Environmental Science Track

Monitoring baseline versus amended soil conditions, tracking pH stabilization, moisture retention rates, and temperature differentials in biochar test plots.

Deliverable: Longitudinal sensor logs & comparative soil health reports.
⚙️

2. Carbon Conversion Efficiency

Engineering & Design Track

Optimizing airflow manifolds, thermal retention in trailer-mounted portable kilns, particulate reduction, and feedstock conversion yields relative to wood moisture content.

Deliverable: Thermal efficiency metrics & open-source CAD refinements.
🌱

3. Nutrient-Charging Trials

Agricultural Science Track

Testing co-composting methods, microbial inoculation rates, and nutrient-loading efficacy of 1,500-lb super sacks prior to field or farm application.

Deliverable: Crop yield impact analysis & optimal charge duration charts.

🏛️ Leveraging Institutional Partnerships (LBCC & OSU Extension)

By establishing clear cooperative agreements with regional academic and extension networks, student researchers gain sanctioned access to field sites for course credit, internships, or capstone projects. In return, the AFTI portal leverages institutional lab facilities (e.g., for precise soil carbon-content analysis or heavy-metal testing in urban/agricultural transition zones) that would otherwise require costly commercial testing.

For Students & Researchers: Real-world field deployment, cross-disciplinary collaboration, resume-building data collection, and direct community impact.
For the Operational Hub: Rigorous third-party validation, enhanced grant application scoring through academic partnerships, and consistent volunteer engagement.
