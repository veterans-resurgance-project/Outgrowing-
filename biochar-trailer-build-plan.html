Mobile Biochar System: Build Plan, Sourcing & Timeline

A phased operational blueprint for fabricating a trailer-mounted mobile pyrolytic kiln system utilizing donated implements, engineering student oversight, and core trainee sweat equity.

📦 1. Materials List & Sourcing Strategy (New vs. Reconditioned/Donated)

To maximize capital efficiency, we leverage a hybrid sourcing model: acquiring the heavy structural chassis via donation or surplus, while investing in critical heat-containment and safety components.

Chassis & Structural (Sourced / Donated)

  • • Dual-Axle Flatbed Trailer: 14ft to 16ft utility trailer (rated 7,000 lbs GVWR) acquired via surplus, donation, or budget reconditioning.
  • • Heavy Steel Enclosures: Salvaged heavy-gauge steel burn boxes or modified 275-gal/300-gal IBC tote inner steel cages.
  • • Tool Racks & Tie-Downs: Recycled angle iron and d-rings for securement.

Core Build & Safety Components (New / Precision)

  • • Refractory Insulation: High-temperature firebrick or ceramic fiber blanket lining for inner walls.
  • • Airflow & Blower System: 12V DC marine bilge blowers + solar panel/battery bank for forced-air top-lit uplift (TLUD).
  • • Safety & Quenching Gear: High-temp welding gloves, heavy-duty fire extinguishers, and water-spray suppression plumbing.

⏱️ 3. Realist Phased Timeline & Cost Estimates

Because material gathering happens concurrently, the build is structured in manageable milestones so operations can scale without premature bottlenecks.

Phase 1: Sourcing & Engineering Blueprints (Weeks 1-4)

Est. Cost: $200 - $500 (Permits/Hardware)

Secure donated flatbed trailer chassis, collect raw steel salvage, and finalize engineering CAD schematics with university advisory partners.

Phase 2: Chassis Refurbishment & Fabrication (Weeks 5-8)

Est. Cost: $800 - $1,500 (Refractory/Steel)

Trailer deck repairs, welding custom burn vessel mountings, installing firebrick insulation lining, and integrating airflow manifolds.

Phase 3: Field Testing & Safety Calibration (Weeks 9-10)

Est. Cost: $300 (Safety gear/hoses)

Initial controlled burn test on storm-fall slash, temperature logging with research teams, and operator safety sign-off.
