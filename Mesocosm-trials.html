Pilot Career Path: Advanced Certification Sponsorship

We believe volunteer hours should translate directly into real-world career growth. Once a pilot logs milestone flight hours mapping VRP project grids, VRP sponsors advanced professional certifications—including ODA Commercial Pesticide Applicator Licenses, Specialized Fertilizer Handlers Endorsements, and Advanced Agricultural Mapping Endorsements.

Bronze Tier (25 Hours) Official VRP Drone Field Specialist Badge & Priority Equipment Access.
Silver Tier (50 Hours) Advanced GIS / Orthomosaic Processing Credential & Letters of Recommendation.
Gold Tier (100 Hours) Full ODA Pesticide / Specialty Certification Sponsorship (in exchange for targeted project mapping).
