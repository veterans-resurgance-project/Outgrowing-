The Greenhouse Council: First Strategy Session

They trickled in from completely different corners of the valley—individuals who, by all normal metrics, would never occupy the same room, let alone form a coalition. Looking around the scarred wooden table set up inside the echoing shell of an empty greenhouse, the assembly looks less like an orderly agricultural crew and more like a tactical team of unlikely heroes. What binds them isn't background or polish, but an unyielding, burning passion to grow and to uplift those whose bodies or circumstances are failing them because of their own grueling origin stories.

Spread out across the table are the initial operational assignments, dossiers of regional elders and struggling homesteaders who need immediate intervention. But one file on the list instantly catches your eye and anchors your attention.

🍎 Target Assignment: The Heritage Orchard Rescue
An elderly widow in the foothills has spent her entire life tending an isolated hillside orchard. Now, with her physical strength failing and poverty closing in, she can no longer keep up with pruning or irrigation. Worse, she carries a profound grief: these aren't ordinary trees. They are rare, living artifact heritage varieties brought across the plains by her grandparents in covered wagons on the Oregon Trail. Through every trial, drought, and displacement, her family kept them alive. Now, she fears centuries of history will die with her.

When you read the file aloud to the table, the arguing stops. Without hesitation, the room shifts. Glances are exchanged, heads nod in unison, and the decision is sealed: She will be the very first farmer the team fights to save.
