The Dual-Funnel Strategic Advantage

By leveraging volunteer-powered and student-led geospatial telemetry (Part 107 drone mapping, multispectral orthomosaics, and soil/fuel load surveys) for grant-funded research partnerships, we establish a zero-cost baseline of ecological data. This protocol governs the secure, compliant transition of that research data into actionable commercial proposals and fee-for-service subcontracts for Cascadia Forestry Solutions.

01

Research Data Mining

Student work-study cells and volunteer pilot teams deploy drones and soil sensors under grant-funded environmental research frameworks (e.g., ODW/USDA Specialty Crop objectives), mapping canopy stress, invasive species, and burn scar fuel loads across partner acreage.

02

The "Soft Foot" Presentation

When approaching private landowners or timber partners, representatives present the completed research telemetry report as a community benefit—demonstrating exact areas of high fire risk, erosion potential, or soil compaction at zero upfront cost.

03

Commercial Subcontract Conversion

Armed with pre-mined data, Cascadia Forestry Solutions provides an immediate, precise bid for hazardous fuel reduction, trail remediation, or soil amendment execution—leveraging trained veteran deployment cells for the paid work.
