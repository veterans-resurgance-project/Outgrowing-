Field Operations Protocol & Mandates

Our veteran crews deploy structured, military-grade discipline to post-wildfire zones across the Pacific Northwest. Every deployment is managed with rigorous safety musters, precise erosion control metrics, and standardized habitat restoration steps to protect vulnerable watersheds.

Burn Scar & Watershed Remediation Checklist

1. Site Assessment & Safety Muster Conduct pre-deployment hazard survey, verify communications relay, and map hydrophobic soil layers.
2. Erosion Control & Contour Log Placement Install straw wattles, log erosion barriers (coir logs), and contour felling across slope lines to mitigate runoff.
3. Soil Amendment & Bio-Inoculation Apply specialized organic soil binders and mycorrhizal amendments to jumpstart microbial recovery in scorched topsoil.
4. Data Logging & Grant-Matching Hours Entry Record operational labor hours, equipment telemetry, and soil recovery samples directly into the Outgrowing-Ag data portal.
