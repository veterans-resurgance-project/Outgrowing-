The Threshold: Remembering the Promise

Six different foster placements. Six different rooms where you tried to figure out the unwritten rules before the ground shifted under your feet again. Without a high school diploma and with doors constantly closing before they even opened, survival meant learning how to look after the little ones who came right behind you—shielding them from the worst of the cold, keeping them safe, and trying to be the anchor you never had.

Before you walked out that final door, you made a silent, unbreakable promise: you were going to make it out, build a real bridge, and come back for them someday. You are their hero, and you can feel the quiet weight of their eyes rooting for you across the miles. This isn't just about carving out a future for yourself; it's about making sure they never have to face the grinding struggle you endured.
