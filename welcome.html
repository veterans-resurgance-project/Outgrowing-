Level Up Your Field Career: How the AFTI Portal Works

Welcome to the platform! Whether you are building an agricultural portfolio, transitioning out of military service, or joining our conservation and watershed crews, your journey here is gamified and verified across three main steps:

1. Earn XP Points

Every time you complete a training module, log verified field hours, or execute operational checklists, you earn Experience Points (XP) that build your real-world professional portfolio.

2. Unlock Required Training

Certain advanced field roles and simulations require foundational knowledge. Completing your required training modules unlocks the keys to higher-level operational tiers.

3. Advance Through Simulation Stages

Move from basic safety and muster protocols all the way to complex land management, agriculture telemetry, and disaster recovery simulations. Your XP and completed modules are your keys to unlocking deeper project responsibilities.
