# Outgrowing-Ag: Workforce Expansion & Self-Sufficiency Addendum ## 1. Executive Summary & Expansion Strategy As *Outgrowing-Ag* transitions from its initial pilot phase into regional expansion, the nonprofit utilizes a dual-engine scaling model: 1. **The Core Self-Sustaining Engine:** Mature deployment cells transition into landowner ownership or ongoing advisory retainers, generating continuous revenue to cover administrative and leadership overhead. 2. **The Workforce Expansion Loop:** New cell creation, multi-track training, and participant stipends are systematically absorbed and funded through targeted workforce development and green-job training grants. --- ## 2. Workforce Grant Integration Framework To scale the number of active grower cells without straining core operating revenues, workforce development grants are leveraged specifically for human capital and training overhead. * **Target Grant Categories:** * State and federal green-job training initiatives. * Agricultural apprenticeship and beginning farmer development programs. * Post-wildfire workforce recovery and forestry stewardship grants. * **Grant Budget Allocation per Expansion Cell:** * **Trainee/Cell Stipends:** Subsidizes the learning period for participants entering the 5-person operational cells. * **Instructional Oversight:** Covers technical mentorship in biochar production, multi-track crop rotation, and soil restoration. * **Safety & Gear:** Allocates funds for personal protective equipment, baseline tools, and field readiness. --- ## 3. 3-Year Revenue & Self-Sufficiency Projections | Revenue / Funding Stream | Year 1 (Startup & Seed) | Year 2 (Growth & Scale) | Year 3 (Transition & Buyout) | Year 4+ (Fully Self-Sustaining) | | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | | **Federal / State Grants** *(Wildfire, Soil, Workforce)* | **$75,000** (75%) | **$50,000** (50%) | **$15,000** (15%) | **$0** (0% - Scaling Mechanics & Long-Term Sustainability * **Capital Recycling:** In Year 3, landowner system buyouts and advisory payback schedules return capital directly to the nonprofit, establishing a revolving fund to build out subsequent sites without requiring fresh startup grants. * **Decoupled Growth:** By utilizing workforce-specific grants exclusively for new labor onboarding, core administrative costs remain protected, allowing the leadership team and permanent infrastructure to scale horizontally across multiple regional properties.
