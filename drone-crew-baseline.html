Step 1: Internal Standard Operating Protocols (SOP)

Before stepping onto a project site, all AFTI crew members must read and understand the following baseline protocols. As a Visual Observer or Ground Operator, you are the eyes and ears of the Pilot in Command (PIC).

Crew Resource Management (CRM)

  • Maintain unbroken visual line of sight (VLOS) with the drone.
  • Use clear, concise radio callouts (e.g., "Traffic, 3 o'clock, high").
  • Never distract the PIC during takeoff, landing, or complex maneuvers.

LiPo Battery & Site Safety

  • Inspect all LiPo batteries for swelling or puncture before charging.
  • Keep the primary landing zone (LZ) clear of unauthorized personnel.
  • Always wear required PPE (high-vis vests, safety glasses) on active watershed/burn scar sites.

Step 2: Obtain Your FAA TRUST Certificate

To legally participate in flight operations, you must pass The Recreational UAS Safety Test (TRUST). This is a free, online, unproctored test provided by the FAA. It takes about 15 minutes and you cannot fail (wrong answers let you try again).

Step 3: How to Apply & Join the Active Roster

  1. Save Your Certificate: Upon finishing the FAA TRUST test, you will be issued a completion certificate. Save this as a PDF or take a clear screenshot. The FAA does not keep a record of it, so do not lose it!
  2. Acknowledge Protocols: Ensure you have read and understood the AFTI SOPs listed in Step 1.
  3. Submit Credentials: Email your TRUST Certificate PDF alongside your AFTI Call Sign/Name to your designated Team Lead or upload it to the AFTI Discord/Comms channel.
  4. Field Orientation: You will be scheduled for a 30-minute on-site physical briefing to verify PPE and radio checks. Once complete, you are officially cleared for active field deployments.
