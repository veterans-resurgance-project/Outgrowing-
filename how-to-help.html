Aiming for December first launch and start of training) The Agricultural Future & Training Initiative (AFTI) thrives on community trust, shared assets, and coordinated mutual aid. By engaging with our regional programs, you actively invest in unhoused veterans, foster-care alumni, and individuals navigating life transitions to establish long-term economic sovereignty.

🛠️ Ways to Invest in the Program

We prefer a strategy centered on small, decentralized community inputs that keep individuals deeply invested in our localized programs.

🔧 Tool & Equipment Donations

We actively seek donations of physical field infrastructure. Contributing small, reliable implements—such as hand tools, grafting knives, seed trays, or irrigation fittings—directly arms our training crews with the assets they depend on.

💻 Specialized Volunteer Support

Our program architecture runs lean. You can contribute your professional time to support ourl efforts. We actively route volunteers into dedicated roles for community outreach, specialized experience or expertise toassist iin future program development.

🌱 Sponsorship & Growth Capital

Corporate partners can back specific regional staging plots or cover stipend draws for next-generation growers. This capital directly builds our Credit Union reserves, funding start-up micro-enterprises at graduation.

🤝 The Value of Shared Credit

✓ Built on Mutual Dignity Every bit of support received goes straight to the frontline field teams. By shifting our focus to decentralized community giving and shared credit, we protect our participants from rigid institutional constraints and establish true agricultural independence.

If you want to coordinate a material tool drop-off, review our active volunteer schedules, or discuss sponsorship boundaries, reach out to our team desks through the community network links.
