# AFTI Mechanical Track: Small Engine & Wildfire Gear Restoration **Course Duration:** Phase 1 Core Infrastructure (4-Week Intensive Module) **Instructional Framework:** Mechanical Resurrection & "Earn-to-Own" Pathway ## Week 1: Shop Orientation, Fluid Dynamics, & Precision Teardown * **Core Competency:** Mastery of the 4 elements of small engine operation (Fuel, Air, Spark, Compression). * **Safety Protocol:** Strict fuel draining, hazardous waste logging, and personal protective equipment (PPE) deployment. * **Hands-on Lab:** Mechanical intake, external pressure washing, and structural inventory mapping of salvaged equipment. * **Milestone:** Complete teardown of a gummed-up 2-stroke chainsaw or pump engine; all components organized into physical parts-bins with an associated digital log entry. ## Week 2: Fuel Delivery Systems & Ultrasonic Restoration * **Core Competency:** Understanding atmospheric pressure differentials, diaphragm carburetors, and fuel line routing. * **Safety Protocol:** Chemical safety management for parts cleaning agents and solvent use. * **Hands-on Lab:** Complete breakdown of Zama/Walbro style carburetors, manual jet clearing, and multi-stage ultrasonic tank cleaning. * **Milestone:** Re-assembly and manual bench-testing of a restored carburetor assembly using fresh needle valves, gaskets, and metering levers. ## Week 3: Electrical Systems, Compression Metrics, & Ignition Spark * **Core Competency:** Flywheel magnetism, coil gapping, solid-state ignition systems, and cylinder wall integrity. * **Safety Protocol:** High-voltage discharge awareness and flywheel puller safety mechanisms. * **Hands-on Lab:** Spark plug gap calibration, coil-to-flywheel spacing using precision feeler gauges, and mechanical compression testing. * **Milestone:** Execution of a verified compression test yielding a minimum of 120 PSI alongside a visible, blue-white spark ignition verification. ## Week 4: Final Tuning, Operational Testing, & Wildfire Defense Deployment * **Core Competency:** High/Low speed needle carb tuning under load, field safety margins, and spark arrestor maintenance. * **Safety Protocol:** Controlled test fire zones, fire extinguisher positioning, and ear/eye barrier enforcement. * **Hands-on Lab:** Re-mounting engines to structural chassis, tuning the fuel mixture for optimal RPM, and structural testing of chain brakes. * **Milestone:** A successful 10-minute continuous run-test under field conditions. Equipment passes directly into the "Earn-to-Own" graduation ledger or is returned to the active equipment pool for local wildfire brush-clearing.
