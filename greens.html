🥬 Market Greens & Produce: Ergonomics & Techniques

Field cutting ergonomics, knife safety, wash-station posture, and cold-water protection standards.

1. Primary Physical Risk Factors

  • Continuous Squatting: Harvesting ground-level crops causes knee joint compression and lower back tightness.
  • Repetitive Knife Motions: Sustained wrist deviation when using harvesting knives or sickles.
  • Cold-Water Immersion Fatigue: Hand stiffness and circulatory constriction during cold-water field washing.

2. Biomechanical & Ergonomic Standards

Keep wrists straight when wielding harvesting knives; allow arm and shoulder motion to push the blade. Utilize rolling harvest carts or straddle benches to maintain supported postures.

3. Step-by-Step Harvest Protocol

  1. Grasp-and-Slice: Secure the top half of the greens bundle lightly; slice cleanly 1/2-inch above soil line in one motion.
  2. Field Trim: Remove yellowing or damaged outer leaves immediately at the bed.
  3. Standardized Bin Packing: Layer greens gently in uniform grid patterns to avoid bruising.
