# OUTGROWING-AG: LANDOWNER PARTNERSHIP MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) **This Memorandum of Understanding ("Agreement")** is entered into as of ____________________ [Date], by and between **Outgrowing-Ag** (a developing Oregon nonprofit organization under a 501(c)(3) framework) ("Organization"), and ________________________________________ ["Landowner"]. --- ## 1. Purpose & Scope The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a collaborative land-sharing partnership for agricultural production, soil health enhancement, and workforce training. The Organization will deploy a supervised crew to manage, plant, maintain, and harvest designated acreage on the Landowner's property, addressing local labor shortages while furthering community restoration goals. ## 2. Property Designation & Access * **Property Location:** __________________________________________________________________ [Address / Tax Lot ID] * **Allocated Acreage:** Approximately ________ acres, as outlined on the attached Site Map (Exhibit A). * **Permitted Access:** Organization personnel, expert trainers, and program participants shall have authorized access to the designated acreage via agreed-upon entry points during daylight hours, or as otherwise coordinated with the Landowner. ## 3. Term & Seasonal Timeline * **Initial Term:** This Agreement shall commence on ____________________ and remain in effect through ____________________. * **Renewal / Overwintering:** This Agreement may be renewed by mutual written consent. Both parties acknowledge and agree that certain crops planted under this initiative may require seasonal transitioning, cover cropping, or overwintering protections extending beyond initial harvest windows, which shall be managed responsibly by the Organization. ## 4. Responsibilities of the Organization The Organization agrees to: * Deploy trained leadership and supervised participant crews to execute land preparation, planting, weed management, and harvesting. * Maintain all necessary hand tools, small equipment, and operational materials required for the agreed cultivation tracks. * Ensure all participants adhere to strict safety protocols, professional conduct guidelines, and environmental stewardship standards. * Keep the designated work area orderly and remove all temporary infrastructure, equipment, or debris upon the conclusion or termination of this Agreement, unless otherwise agreed in writing. ## 5. Responsibilities of the Landowner The Landowner agrees to: * Grant peaceful and unimpeded access to the designated acreage for the duration of the term. * Provide reasonable access to available water infrastructure or utilities required for irrigation, as specified in Exhibit B. * Furnish timely notice of any scheduled chemical applications, heavy machinery operations, or property maintenance nearby that could impact the cultivation zone. ## 6. Harvest Distribution & Yield Harvested yield from the designated acreage shall be allocated in accordance with the following agreed structure: * [%] Distributed to local food security initiatives / community distribution. * [%] Allocated to participating crew members and project supporters. * [%] Provided directly to the Landowner as yield share or site consideration. *(Specific percentages to be mutually agreed upon prior to planting).* ## 7. Liability & Indemnity The Organization operates under structured project safety frameworks. The Organization agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Landowner from any direct claims, damages, or liabilities arising strictly out of the negligent acts or omissions of the Organization's participants or staff while on the property, ordinary wear and natural agricultural activities excepted. --- ### Signatures **For Outgrowing-Ag (Organization):** Signature: ___________________________________ Printed Name: Donald Laroy Truex Title: Executive Director / Founder Date: _________________ **For Landowner:** Signature: ___________________________________ Printed Name: ________________________________ Title: _______________________________________ Date: _________________
