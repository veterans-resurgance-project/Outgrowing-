🔬 Research Data Collection, Assembly & Relay Protocol

To meet the rigorous empirical standards of university extension partners (such as the Oregon State University Extension Service), raw farm operations are transformed into publication-grade agricultural research data through standardized metrology, dual-operator chain-of-custody, and longitudinal tracking.

📐 1. Metrology & Calibration

Precision Over Anecdote: Environmental and yield parameters are never logged as subjective adjectives. Sensors undergo bi-weekly baseline calibration against USDA standards.

  • Substrate moisture measured via TDR moisture meters.
  • Temperature & RH recorded with certified hygrometers.
  • Biomass yields recorded in precise metric grams (g/sq.ft).
Standard: USDA Ag Experiment Station

👥 2. Chain-of-Custody

Dual-Operator Sign-Off: To eliminate single-observer bias and fulfill academic peer-review principles, every daily log requires a verified working pair (Minimum 2 operators).

  • Primary Operator records telemetry.
  • Secondary Operator audits and countersigns.
  • Timestamped audit trail prevents backfilling.
Standard: Double-Blind Field Auditing

📊 3. Longitudinal Tracking

Batch & Lifecycle Indexing: Data points are linked to specific batch serial numbers (e.g., #MSH-2026-09) allowing researchers to analyze multi-generational crop response.

  • Independent Variable tracking (inputs/amendments).
  • Dependent Variable output analysis (yield/brix).
  • Anomaly root-cause tracking & 14-day re-test.
Standard: Relational Agronomic Schema
