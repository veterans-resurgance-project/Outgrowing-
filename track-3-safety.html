Module Overview & Protocol Standards

Designed around American Red Cross standards and wilderness trauma protocols. Recruits learn rapid triage, tourniquet application, heat exhaustion mitigation, and remote radio emergency communication procedures.

Unit 1: Wilderness Triage & Bleeding Control

Prioritizing casualties in low-resource environments. Proper windlass tourniquet placement, wound packing techniques, and managing hypothermia during extended field delays.

Unit 2: Environmental Hazard Mitigation

Recognizing stages of heat stroke versus dehydration, bee/asp sting anaphylaxis response, and safe evacuation signaling using GPS coordinates and mirror/smoke vectors.
