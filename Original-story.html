The Reality of the Street

The heavy doors of the youth center click shut behind you. You are 18, standing on the cold sidewalk with nothing but a single bag. The sky is turning a dark charcoal purple, the temperature is plumetting, and the city feels vast and expensive. You have no safety net, no family couch to crash on, and your phone has no active service.

It is hard to dream of growing a field of crops when your fingers are numb right now. But inside your bag is a single matching code blueprint for an agricultural land trust. To reach that soil, you have to survive tonight. Your first test is navigating your way to local advocates.
