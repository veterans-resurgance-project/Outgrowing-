Legal & Intellectual Property Framework

Research Data Partnership & MOU Structure

A standardized template and governance model for protecting Shared Harvest data during academic and private collaborations.

Core Protection Principles

This framework ensures that any university, extension office, or private entity partnering with the incubator acknowledges Shared Harvest as the sole proprietor of raw operational metrics, participant telemetry, and proprietary site blueprints. Collaborators gain analytical access and co-publishing credentials without gaining ownership of the foundational data assets.

Standardized MOU Data Clause (Draft Template)

You can insert the following boilerplate language into future research or grant partnerships:

SECTION: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & DATA OWNERSHIP 1. Ownership of Raw Data: All raw data, telemetry logs, participant milestone records, and proprietary agricultural Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) generated within the Shared Harvest Incubator ecosystem remain the exclusive intellectual property of Shared Harvest ("The Enterprise"). 2. Limited License for Research: Shared Harvest grants the Collaborating Entity [University/Partner Name] a non-exclusive, revocable license to analyze, review, and reference aggregated or anonymized research data solely for the approved educational or scientific evaluation scope outlined in Appendix A. 3. Attribution & Co-Authorship: Any publications, academic papers, books, or public presentations utilizing insights derived from Enterprise operations must explicitly credit Shared Harvest as the originating research body. Commercial resale or unauthorized third-party distribution of raw datasets is strictly prohibited.

Why This Protects Your Future Expansion

By codifying this into every agreement upfront, you position Shared Harvest not as a passive subject of study, but as an active intellectual partner. When the time comes to compile your findings into a book, a standardized consulting package, or an expanded regional grant, your proprietary data remains entirely under your control.
