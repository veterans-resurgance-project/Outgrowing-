Welcome & Self-Paced Study Strategy

Welcome to the AFTI training pipeline. This module prepares operators for the FAA Part 107 Aeronautical Knowledge Test and specialized commercial operations, including ecological restoration, aerial seeding, and watershed telemetry mapping.

You are encouraged to work through the materials and pretests at your own pace. Dive into the official government handbooks and industry guides linked below to master each core competency before moving ahead to field deployment.

Core Question Breakdown & Operational Fit

Module 1

Regulations & Operations

Covers FAA Part 107 speed caps (87 knots / 100 mph), sobriety rules (8-hour window), cloud clearances, $500+ incident reporting thresholds, and Pilot in Command (PIC) accountability.

Module 2

Airspace & Charts

Focuses on controlled airspace (Class B, C, D, E), LAANC / FAA DroneZone authorization workflows, sectional chart symbols, 3 statute mile visibility rules, and TFR NOTAMs.

→ Launch Pretest 2
Module 3

Weather & Performance
