Field Outreach Toolkit

Landowner Partnership Script & Talking Points

Objective: To secure initial land shares or leases from local property owners and agricultural producers by positioning Outgrowing-Ag as a professional, zero-burden solution to regional labor shortages, while introducing our supervised dual-track workforce model [1].

1. The Opening Hook (Establishing Common Ground)

"Hello [Name], I’m reaching out on behalf of Outgrowing-Ag. We know firsthand how fierce the agricultural labor shortages are right now across the Willamette Valley, and many property owners are sitting on valuable acreage that’s tough to keep managed [1].

We provide a structured, supervised workforce solution designed to take the heavy lifting completely off your plate while bringing idle land into productive use [1]."

2. The Core Value Proposition (The "Zero-Burden" Pitch)

  • What we do: "We bring an experienced core team that handles initial site setup, infrastructure management, planting, and harvest execution [1]."
  • How we protect your time: "You aren't managing extra labor or fielding day-to-day issues. Our team operates under a strict professional project-management framework—we handle the logistics, and you get your crops in the ground and harvested on time [1]."

3. Addressing Key Concerns (Proactive Reassurance)

  • On Liability & Control:
    "Everything we do is governed by clear written agreements, defined site boundaries, and strict safety protocols. Our workers operate under direct supervision at all times [1]."
  • On Tax Deferrals (EFU / Farm-Forest Land):
    "Our agricultural and soil health tracks actively support active land management, helping ensure your property maintains its intended agricultural utility and compliance [1]."
  • On Crop Timing & Overwintering:
    "We build our planting and harvest schedules with seasonal transitions in mind—including cover crops and overwintering strategies—ensuring the land is properly cared for year-round [1]."

4. The Broader Mission (Community Impact)

"Beyond solving immediate labor and harvest needs, our crews are part of a structured program that provides dignified employment pathways, skills training, and stipends for individuals—including veterans and underemployed community members—rebuilding their workforce footing [1]."

5. The Closing Call to Action

"We’d love to walk the property with you, look at a small test plot or designated acreage, and see how a partnership could benefit both your land and our community mission. Are you available for a quick site visit sometime next week [1]?"
