🛠️ Main Farm Succession & Labor Strategy

How Will You Replace the Twins' Labor?

Now that the twins have carved out their own 5-acre ventures, the main farm has officially lost two full-time pairs of hands. You must adapt your strategy or invest in replacement labor to maintain your acreage output.

👥 Strategy 1: Seasonal Hired Hands

Hire External Farm Labor

Bring on part-time workers to handle intense weeding, picking, and packing.

Economic Impact:
• Adds an annual **-$8,000 wage overhead**.
• **Benefit:** Allows you to keep your high-yield, labor-intensive specialty crops fully planted without burning out.
🚜 Strategy 2: Mechanized Pivot

Pivot Main Farm to Low-Labor

Downsize hand-harvested specialty lines and shift toward bulk, mechanized crops.

Economic Impact:
• Keeps wage overhead at **$0**.
• **Trade-off:** Lowers gross revenue potential on main acreage in exchange for total self-sufficiency and low burnout risk.
