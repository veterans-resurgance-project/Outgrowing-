Industrial Motion Study & Harvesting Efficiency

In specialty crop harvesting (berries, snap/pole beans, bush fruit), hand speed is not achieved by rushing; it is achieved by eliminating wasted motion. Time studies break down picking cycles into four primary phases: Reach & Search (15%), Grasp & Detach (60%), Deposit (15%), and Container Transit (10%).

1. Two-Handed Palm Batching

Novices reach, grab one berry/pod, and carry it back to the bucket. Professionals pick 3 to 5 items into their palm using a light roll-and-hold technique before moving their arm toward the collection container. This reduces hand travel distance by up to 70%.

2. Roll-Thumb Detachment

To prevent skin tears on blueberries or stem pulls on fresh-market snap beans, use the thumb pad to gently roll the pedicel stem against the index finger. Never pull straight away from the vine or cane.
