Step 1 & 2

Conceptualization & Grid Translation

Learn how to take a rough, unscaled hand drawing and map it onto a rigorous 1-foot grid layout, factoring in companion plant synergy and crop spacing before digital drafting.

Module 1.1: Square-Foot Scaling

Converting freehand shapes into rigid 1ft x 1ft quadrants. Calculating exact plant density quotas per square based on mature canopy width.

Action Item: Overlay a coordinate grid onto the participant's original pencil sketch.
Module 1.2: Companion Zonation

Pairing deep-root crops with shallow-root companions and embedding natural deterrent zones (alliums/herbs) along border edges.

Action Item: Color-code plant families to prevent nutrient depletion and pest clustering.
Step 3 & 4

Professional Graphic Presentation & Drafting

Transition the scaled pencil drawing into a clean visual asset embedded with workforce logistics, tool-staging zones, and cell pathways.

Module 2.1: Digital Clean-Up

Inking boundary lines, generating precise scale bars, and implementing standardized legend keys for public viewing.

Module 2.2: Operational Overlays

Marking 5-person cell access lanes, biochar application zones, and organic waste collection loops on the master graphic.

Step 5 & 6

Narrative & Commercial Pitch Packaging

Write the accompanying operational roadmap detailing the 3-year shared harvest timeline and the transparent cost-plus buyout formula for prospective landowners.

Module 3.1: 3-Year Milestone Timeline

Drafting the phased evolution from Year 1 (Foundation & Build) through Year 2 (Yield & Pop-Up Markets) to Year 3 (Handover Readiness).

Module 3.2: Cost-Plus Value Statement

Explaining the buyout structure (Equipment + Labor + Engineering Margin) to prove system design validity.

Step 7 & 8: Final Output

On-Site Placard & Advertising Generator

Preview and generate the exact text layout used for outdoor weather-resistant signage mounted on farm perimeters to attract participants and buyers.
