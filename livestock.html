Parlor Hygiene & Herd Welfare Operations

Dairy and livestock operations run 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Maintaining high milk quality (low somatic cell count) requires flawless milking parlor routine, consistent TMR feed pusher schedules, and early symptom detection.

1. Pre-Milking Prep Routine

The standard sequence: Strip teats to inspect for abnormal milk, pre-dip with germicidal solution, allow 30 seconds contact time, wipe completely dry with single-use towel, and attach clusters within 60–90 seconds to leverage natural oxytocin release.

2. Early Health Scouting

Observing cattle behavior during feed distribution. Animals hanging back, showing reduced rumination, or elevated respiratory rates are flagged for immediate health checks.
