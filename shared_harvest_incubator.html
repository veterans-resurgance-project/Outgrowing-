The 3-Year Shared Harvest Loop

From Pencil Design to Turnkey Farm Acquisition

Participants begin their journey by submitting hand-drawn pencil plot designs tailored to their specific passion—whether that is specialty cut flowers, native pollinator habitats, or gourmet mushroom cultivation. When a landowner selects a design, a specialized 5-person cell is deployed to execute it over a 3-year shared harvest program, educating participants into complete system operators. At term completion, the landowner has the option to exercise a cost-plus buyout, proving our systems design and funding the next cohort.

Year 1: Foundation & Build

Cell deployment, fire-buffer clearing, biochar integration, and initial pencil-design layout implementation on site.

Year 2: Yield & Operation

Cross-training rotation, pop-up micro-business market stands, and peak agricultural yield management.

Year 3: Maturity & Buyout

Full operational handover readiness, system auditing, and cost-plus sell-back evaluation for permanent farm adoption.
