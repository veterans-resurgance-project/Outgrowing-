SUBJECT: Financial, Tax, and Grant Accounting Framework for Trainee Stipends, Upskilling Scholarships, and Executive Compensation Phase-In

1. Executive Summary & Purpose

As our organization scales through its early operational phases, personnel and participants will transition across three distinct tiers: (1) Initial Training Stipends, (2) Advanced Upskilling/Certifications, and (3) Full-Time Executive Employment. To ensure complete audit readiness, IRS compliance (avoiding employee/independent contractor misclassification and stipend-to-wage leakage), and institutional grant compliance, this memorandum establishes the mandatory General Ledger (GL) segregation and governance rules.

3. Detailed Operational & Accounting Procedures

A. Handling Tier 1 (Stipends)

  • No Payroll Tax Withholding: Because stipends are designed to offset living expenses during introductory service/training rather than act as hourly wages, they must not be processed through standard W-2 payroll.
  • Audit Trail: Each stipend disbursement requires an active Training MOU signed by the participant, tracking attendance or milestone completion.

B. Handling Tier 2 (Upskilling & Certifications)

  • Direct-to-Vendor Model: When a participant or staff member undergoes specialized training (e.g., CDL testing, heavy equipment certification, specialized ag-tech courses), invoice payments must go directly from the nonprofit to the educational institution or testing facility.
  • Service Commitment Agreement: To protect the organization's investment, any specialized upskilling grant or scholarship exceeding $500 must be backed by a written agreement requiring the recipient to complete a designated period of service with the organization or its partner network upon completion.

C. Handling Tier 3 (Executive Phase-In & Catch-Up Draws)

  • Zero Artificial Accrual: Per our Board Resolution, the $6,500/month executive salary is strictly deferred. No unearned liability shall be placed on the balance sheet during lean months.
  • The Waterline Rule: The 90-day operating reserve (excluding executive pay) must remain untouched. Catch-up draws for deferred executive pay are only triggered when surplus cash exceeds this waterline, and cumulative annual pay cannot exceed $78,000.
