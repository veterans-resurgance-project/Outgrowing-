Save the Farm: Partner & Extension Framework

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and stakeholder guide outlines how the "Save the Farm" gamified training module captures verifiable empirical data, drives extension research engagement, and secures grant compliance for regional agricultural partners across Linn County and the Willamette Valley.

1. Value Proposition for Extension Agents & Researchers

University and OSU Extension services require documented outreach, active trial participation, and educational contact hours to satisfy their land-grant mission. This platform functions as an automated force multiplier:

  • Research Garden Integration: Trainees actively log physical hours (trenching, soil amendment, frost protection) at extension research plots, providing free labor while learning validated agronomic techniques.
  • Data Capture: Automatically logs participant demographics, geographic distribution (zip codes), and training hours without burdensome paperwork.
  • Adoption Metrics: Demonstrates high-rate adoption of specialty crops, biochar soil conditioning, and climate-resilient practices among beginning farmers.

2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for Farm Advisors

Advisors can utilize individual game levels as standardized training modules during mentor-mentee site visits:

  • Level 1-2 (Diagnostics & Cash Flow): Use as an intake interview checklist for distressed landowners seeking financial recovery pathways.
  • Level 3 (Soil Armor): Standardize the application protocols for integrating biochar from regional forest fire-scar remediation into small farm plots.
  • Level 4 (Four Seasons): Guide hands-on field scheduling across seasonal milestones to ensure participants grasp the physical reality of farm management.
  • Level 5-6 (Distribution & Leases): Utilize standardized MOU templates for share-cropping and micro-farm leasing once credit points are redeemed.

3. Grant Reviewer Compliance & Metrics Matrix

When submitting grant proposals, this framework answers every core funder requirement:

Grant Requirement Game Mechanism Verified Output
Unduplicated Reach Level 1 Intake Form Exact participant name, zip code, and baseline demographics.
Educational Hours Levels 2 & 3 Logging Timestamped training and soil science hours.
Food Security Yield Level 5 Distribution Log Pounds of produce allocated to regional food banks.
Land Access / Leasing Level 6 Vanguard Redemption Signed share-crop agreements and micro-farm leases.
