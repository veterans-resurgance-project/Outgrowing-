BCRCC Drone Grid Telemetry & Mapping

Time Window: Bi-Weekly Saturdays (Weather Permitting)

Aerial grid mapping with Bob Kaufman and BCRCC to track invasive blackberry cover and soil compaction across Waterloo Park & project sites.

Biochar Production & Garden Soil Exchange

Time Window: Monthly Work Days

Convert hazardous forestry fuels into high-grade biochar. Volunteers receive a complimentary bag of charged biochar for home gardens!
