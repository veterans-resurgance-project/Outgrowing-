Staff Training Manual: USDA LFPP

1. What This Grant Is & Why We Use It

The USDA Local Food Promotion Program (LFPP) funds e-commerce infrastructure, processing tracks, direct-to-consumer pipelines, and local food distribution networks. For AFTI, this grant powers our Digital Sales & Processing Track—allowing tech recruits to bridge generational grower wisdom with digital markets.

2. How the Operating Model Works in Practice

  • The Generational Bridge: Senior farm stewards manage growing and physical land operations, while our digital-native crew handles e-commerce, pre-orders, digital marketing, and processing oversight.
  • Handling & Processing Percentage: A modest handling fee (typically 10–15%) from digital sales and processed goods is reinvested back into platform tech infrastructure and processing logistics.
  • Early Cash Flow: Digital pre-orders capture retail capital before crops leave the ground, giving host farms reliable financial predictability.

3. Step-by-Step Staff Operating Guide

  • Step 1 (Compile Metrics): Pull active e-commerce pipeline projections, farm stand digital sales data, and customer reach logs.
  • Step 2 (Outline Processing Protocols): Attach specifications for value-added processed yield tracks (dried goods, packaged foods, specialty items) and fee schedules.
  • Step 3 (Attach Digital Agreements): Include signed copies of Section 3 from the Cooperative Package covering digital market representation and sales splits.
  • Step 4 (Finalize Infrastructure Budget): Verify line items for e-commerce software scaling, processing/packaging equipment, and digital logistics.
