Social Support & Early-Stage Pathways

Bridge & Farm Initiative

Connecting community resources, emergency stabilization, and tangible milestones for early-stage participants.

Phase 1: Survival & Stabilization (First 30 Days)

Focused on initial intake, baseline attendance, and completing core safety orientations. Participants receive immediate, dignity-affirming support including professional gear/PPE kits, work boots, and emergency transit or food vouchers secured through our advisory network and social service partners to eliminate early survival stress.

Phase 2: Skill Mastery & Milestone Kits (60 Days)

Triggered upon successful rotation through initial production systems. Earned rewards include portable utility tool kits, branded field gear, and sponsorship for official third-party certifications (such as OSHA-10, food handler cards, or forestry safety credentials) to build verifiable external employability.

Phase 3: The Proof-of-Effort Threshold (90 Days)

Marked by the completion of heavy foundational builds and reliable performance during peak operations. Participants unlock regular fresh produce and mushroom shares for their households, access to digital administration tools, and direct entry into paid operations leadership or piece-rate harvest-share pools.
