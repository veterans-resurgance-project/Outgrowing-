# PROGRAM ARCHITECTURE & GRANT STRATEGY: AFTI AGRICULTURAL TRAINING & LAND-LINKAGE PORTAL ## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy The AFTI Portal is a living enterprise ecosystem that bridges barrier-free entry (supporting unhoused individuals, at-risk youth, and veterans) with high-level commercial agricultural employment, academic credit, and independent farm stewardship. * **The Paradigm Shift:** Moving away from deficit-based charity models, the program empowers participants as community "heroes" and stewards. By solving real municipal and ecological challenges (e.g., public land restoration, watershed management, community food security), participants build verifiable public recognition and self-worth. * **Core Impact Metric:** Transformation from systemic dependency to long-term economic independence via commercial labor matching, land leasing pipelines ([Oregon Farm Link](https://oregonfarmlink.org)), and USDA framework integration. ## 2. Structural & Operational Architecture * **The Foundational Core (10-Person Pilot):** * **2 Work-Study Instructor Assistants:** Academic bridges linking field telemetry to higher education credit pathways. * **5 Veterans Operations Leaders:** Anchoring field discipline, commercial farm multi-day labor standards, and seasonal hand-offs. * **3 Specialist Aspiring Farmers:** Driving niche production nodes (mycology, soil microbiology, specialized crop propagation). * **The Four-Season Competency Cycle:** * **Winter (Phase 1):** Planning, infrastructure maintenance, safety orientation, and baseline theory. * **Spring (Phase 2):** Bed preparation, cover cropping, direct seeding, and live soil/moisture telemetry logging. * **Summer (Phase 3):** High-intensity crop cultivation, drone/watershed aerial telemetry sorties, and community harvest share distribution. * **Fall (Phase 4):** Final harvest, seed saving, bed winterization, and peer-to-peer cohort hand-off. ## 3. The Waitlist Service Pipeline & Grant-Matching Engine * **Surge Capacity & "Heroic Gestures":** Waitlist participants are immediately integrated into civic service projects (e.g., firewood delivery, public land cleanup, site prep) before formal onboarding. * **Non-Cash Match Generation:** Volunteer hours convert into certified in-kind matching funds for institutional grants (such as OWEB or conservation district programs) using standardized labor values. * **Natural Talent Discovery:** Field performance during service projects acts as an organic filter, identifying mechanics, builders, drone operators, and future leaders naturally. ## 4. Academic & Economic Mobility Bridges * **Credit for Prior Learning (CPL) & CWE:** Multi-year digital portfolios (telemetry logs, sweat equity ledgers) map directly to community college Cooperative Work Experience (CWE) credits. * **Federal Work-Study (FWS):** Advanced cohort members are integrated as paid peer-educators and extension field assistants through college work-study allocations. * **Commercial Field Bonus Multipliers:** Work completed on verified commercial partner farms yields a 1.5x to 2.0x XP multiplier, fast-tracking experienced workers into leadership and aligning with regional farm labor demands.
