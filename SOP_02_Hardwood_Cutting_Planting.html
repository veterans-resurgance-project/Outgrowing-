SOP #02 • Volunteer Training Module

Live-Staking & Hardwood Cutting Installation

Target Window: Late Fall or Early Spring (Soil Moisture Peak)

Volunteer XP +100 XP

Event Overview & Objective

Live staking installs dormant woody cuttings directly into stream banks and fire-damaged slopes. Cuttings take root quickly, providing immediate structural soil stabilization and creating canopy cover to suppress invasive weeds.

Site Safety

  • Watch for loose ash, slippery banks, and burnt root voids
  • Wear sturdy ankle-support boots & eye protection
  • Establish 2-person buddy teams on steep slopes

Required Tools & PPE

  • Rebar pilot stakes & dead-blow/sledge hammers
  • Rubber mallets or wood blocks (protect stake tops)
  • Water buckets to keep cuttings hydrated
  • Flagging tape & planting bags

Step-by-Step Planting Protocol

1. Keep Cuttings Hydrated Never let roots or cuts dry in the sun. Keep cuttings submerged in buckets or wrapped in wet burlap until the exact moment of planting.
2. Drive Rebar Pilot Hole In rocky or compacted burn soil, drive a rebar stake 2 feet into the ground to create a pilot hole. Never hammer live stakes directly into hard ground—it splits the bark and kills the stem.
3. Orient & Insert (Angled End Down) Insert the ANGLED END DOWN into the hole. Ensure at least 2/3 of the stake is underground, leaving 1/3 (with at least 2 viable buds) exposed above soil level.
4. Tamp & Seal Soil Air Pockets Firmly tamp soil around the base of the stake with your boot. Air pockets around the underground stem will dry out developing root nodes.
Credential Issued: Live-Staking & Riparian Planting Specialist
