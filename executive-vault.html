💰 Section A: Controlled Fiscal Structures

1. Trainee Stipend & Capital Accumulation Split

To protect participant stability while actively bootstrapping their future independence, all programmatic funding channels enforce a rigid disbursement split:

  • $1,100 Monthly Active Draw: Distributed immediately onto secure electronic platforms to cover baseline housing, local transportation, and immediate food lines.
  • $1,100 Monthly Deferred Compensation: Held securely in local credit union reserve portfolios. This accrued balance compiles continuously, unlocking vital startup capital for independent land shares at graduation.

2. Heavy Equipment Leasing Exemption Policy

To shelter the organization from extreme upfront debt traps, Outgrowing-Ag enforces a 0% equipment leasing stance. 100% of tractors, tillers, and heavy machinery operations are contracted out to existing local operators at fixed flat rates, recycling financial capital directly back into the local community economy.

📋 Section B: Program Charter Framework

1. Non-Punitive Educational Parameters

All simulation paths and field test software must prevent hard failure states. Sub-optimal choice configurations must award partial points (e.g., Middle Choice = 25 XP, Bad Choice = 0 XP) and provide direct, actionable explanations of the consequences, immediately routing participants onto a functional recovery track.

2. Regional Symbiosis Directive

Crews are systematically deployed to restore under-utilized, compacted, or wildfire-affected parcels owned by aging producers or widows. This locks in mutual economic revitalization: landowners receive a 30%–40% equitable share-crop yield value, while next-generation growers bank vital production track records.
