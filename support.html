Community Support & Wish List

Building Regional Capacity Together

Supporting post-wildfire watershed restoration, agroforestry training, and veteran professional development across Linn County and the Willamette Valley.

Tier 1: Community Participation & Workshop Gear

Servicable Items & Everyday Tools

We welcome contributions from local community members looking to pitch in. This includes everyday field gear as well as used equipment ready for a second life in our maintenance training tracks:

  • Used & Servicable Equipment: Used chainsaws, brush cutters, and small gas equipment that may need minor tune-ups or servicing.
  • Hand Tools & Hardware: Hand trowels, pruners, loppers, sturdy hammers, and heavy-duty tarps.
  • Safety & Storage: Clean 5-gallon buckets with lids, durable work gloves, and clear safety glasses.
  • Field Hydration: Clean coolers, insulated thermoses, and reusable water jugs.
Tier 2: Shared Infrastructure & Logistics

Heavy Transport & Communication Gear

Utilized by our core group of trainees across both programs for regional transport and team safety:

  • Work Truck & Flatbed Trailer: Essential for hauling restoration materials, plants, and equipment between sites.
  • Field Communication Gear: Rugged two-way radios and satellite units for remote state forest operations.
  • Regional Fuel & Maintenance Support: Gas cards and upkeep assistance for transit across Linn County project hubs.
Tier 3: Specialized Program Tracks

AFTI Agroforestry & VRP Restoration Units

As our teams branch out into specialized agricultural units and restoration bids, we maintain registries for:

  • Greenhouse & Nursery Supplies: Propagation trays, seed-starting soils, and automated drip-irrigation lines.
  • sUAS Drone Fleet Hardware: FAA Part 107 drone units and field tablets for vegetative canopy tracking.
  • Restoration Tools: Pulaski wildfire tools, McLeod units, and heavy-duty brush clearing gear.

Connect Regarding Contributions

Have tools, equipment, or questions about how to support our veteran work crews? Reach out to coordinate drop-offs or partnership opportunities.
