📐 Square Foot Gardening (SFG) Method & Plant Science

Developed by Mel Bartholomew, the Square Foot Gardening system organizes raised beds into 1x1 foot grids, eliminating single-row spacing waste. By utilizing a "Mel's Mix" soil substrate (1/3 vermiculite, 1/3 peat moss, 1/3 blended compost), roots experience loose aeration, optimal water retention, and high nutrient availability without compacted walking paths.

Companion Planting Synergy: Grouping mutually beneficial species enhances pest deterrence (e.g., marigolds releasing root exudates discouraging nematodes), optimizes vertical space (vining legumes fixing atmospheric nitrogen for heavy-feeding brassicas or solanaceae), and attracts beneficial pollinators.
