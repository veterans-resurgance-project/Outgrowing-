Preventing Methane ( CH 4 ​ ) and Smoke Pollution In a traditional open slash pile, smoldering biomass releases unburned hydrocarbons, toxic particulates, and methane ( CH 4 ​ ), which is 28 to 36 times more potent than CO 2 ​ as a greenhouse gas over a 100-year timescale. In the double-barrel nested setup, the volatile wood gas is pulled into the hot combustion gap (annulus) and burned as fuel. This internal re-combustion destroys particulate smoke and converts volatile hydrocarbons into thermal energy to drive the pyrolysis reaction. B. The Sequestration Yield ( CO 2 ​ Equivalent) 1 lb of pure biochar locks up roughly 2.5 to 3.0 lbs of atmospheric CO 2 ​ equivalent ( CO 2 ​ e) because it freezes elemental carbon that plants pulled from the air during growth. When left on the forest floor, 90–95% of that carbon returns to the atmosphere as CO 2 ​ within 5 to 10 years via microbial decay. By pyrolyzing it into recalcitrant carbon, over 80% of that solid carbon remains locked for 100+ to 1,000+ years. 3. Monetizing the Carbon Benefit (Carbon Removal Credits) In current carbon markets (such as Puro.earth or Verra), durable biochar carbon removal (CORCs) trades at $120 to $180+ per metric ton of CO 2 ​ e sequestered. 1 Ton of Biochar Produced ≈ 2.5 to 3.0 Tons of CO 2 ​ e Removed. This adds an extra $300 to $500 of value per ton of biochar produced on top of the physical value of the char. Summary for Demonstrations & Grant Proposals When presenting this to agencies or students, you can frame the double-barrel method with three simple facts: Energy Efficiency: It reuses its own smoke as fuel, eliminating open air smoke and smoldering methane. Economic Value: It converts free, hazardous fuel-load slash into 15–$30/$\text{ft}^3 worth of environmental filtration media. Permanent Carbon Storage: It locks carbon in a solid, stable form for centuries rather than letting it rot or burn into greenhouse gases.
