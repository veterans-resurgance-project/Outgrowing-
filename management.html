Precision Irrigation, Fertigation & IPM Scouting

Field management bridges field science with labor leadership. By utilizing soil moisture probe telemetry and integrated pest management (IPM) thresholds, field supervisors optimize yield uniformities while streamlining labor pacing.

1. Smart Drip Fertigation

Monitoring soil tension values (centibars) to calculate exact evapotranspiration (ETc) replacement. Managing EC (Electrical Conductivity) during bloom to optimize fruit firmness.

2. Economic Threshold Scouting

Calculating pest populations per leaf/trap before executing pesticide applications. Utilizing biological controls to minimize crop re-entry delay intervals for field workers.
