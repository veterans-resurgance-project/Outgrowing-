Mushroom Cultivation Task List

Focus: Humidity control, misting cycles, CO2 exhaust, and contamination checks.

Daily Routines

Monitor and log fruiting chamber humidity (target 85–92%) and temperature.
Inspect automated misting cycles; manually mist block surfaces if dry.
Run exhaust fans / check passive air exchange to purge CO2 build-up.
Morning & evening harvest inspection (pick before cap edges curl upward).

Weekly Routines

Inspect incubation room grain spawn / sawdust blocks for trichoderma mold.
Clear out exhausted blocks, compost spent substrate, and sanitize shelving.
Package harvested mushrooms into vented containers and stage in cold storage (34–38°F).

Apiary & Pollination Task List

Focus: Colony health, water access, swarm prevention, and varroa monitoring.

Daily Routines

Observe morning flight activity at hive entrances (pollen loads & orientation).
Check and refill apiary water stations (ensure clean floats).
Quick visual scan for ant trails, yellowjacket pressure, or hive beetle entry.

Weekly Routines

Full 7–10 day spring/summer hive inspection (check for queen cells & brood pattern).
Conduct Varroa mite monitoring (sticky board count or alcohol wash).
Build frames, wire foundation sheets, or maintain extracting equipment.

Specialty Cut Flowers Task List

Focus: Dawn harvesting, pest scouting, immediate hydration, and trellising.

Daily Routines

Morning/dusk harvesting of turgid stems (no midday cutting).
Scout underside of foliage for aphids, thrips, and powdery mildew.
Strip lower foliage immediately; place stems in clean buckets with cool water/preservative.

Weekly Routines

Direct-seed or transplant succession rounds (sunflowers, zinnias, etc.).
Hand-weed delicate root zones and test drip irrigation lines.
Adjust Florida weave or netting upward to prevent stem lodging from wind/rain.

Fast-Growing Greens Task List

Focus: Consistent moisture, temperature control, sequential seeding, and wash/spin cycles.

Daily Routines

Monitor soil moisture; run light overhead irrigation or sub-surface watering.
Adjust high-tunnel sidewalls or deploy shade cloth if ambient temp exceeds 75°F.
Scout for flea beetles, slugs, and aphid pressure on tender leaf crops.

Weekly Routines

Sow new trays or direct-seed beds on a 7-day rotation schedule.
Harvest mature greens, submerge in triple-wash chill tanks, and spin-dry.
Immediately clear old root stubble post-harvest, amend with compost, and re-fluff beds.
