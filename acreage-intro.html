📋 Core Simulation Mechanics & Goals

1. Track Selection

Choose between a Bootstrapping Track ($8k starting cash with Shared Harvest subsidies) or an Independent Track ($25k starting cash with total autonomy).

2. Acre Allocation

Distribute your 10 land plots across high-yield enterprises: Leafy Greens, Root Veggies, High-Tunnel Berries, Mushrooms, Apiary, and Cut Flowers.

3. Seasonal Events

Navigate variable climate shocks (like late spring frost) and take advantage of bumper harvest markets or advisory mitigation protocols.

🎯 Your 5-Year Objective

Manage overhead, maintain cash flow through multi-year crop cycles (such as establishing perennial berries), and successfully scale your enterprise across 5 full operational seasons to unlock your final graduation report and advance to the Shared Harvest Hub.
